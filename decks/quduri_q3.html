Book of Endowment (Waqf)

Waqf is the dedication of a property's benefit to a charitable purpose. Al-Qudūrī sets out the Imām's three-way disagreement with his two students on when the founder's ownership ends (judicial ruling or death-bequest; mere declaration; or appointing a custodian and handing over), then the validity conditions (undivided shares, a perpetual end-beneficiary, immovables), the effects once valid (no sale, repair comes first), and finally mosques and public facilities, which have their own thresholds. Later Hanafī fatwā generally leaned toward Abū Yūsuf's easier view that waqf becomes binding on declaration; the text itself only reports the three positions.
When does the founder's ownership of a waqf end according to Abū Ḥanīfa?
لا يزول ملك الواقف عن الوقف عند أبي حنيفة إلا أن يحكم به الحاكم أو يعلقه بموته
Not by his words alone; only when a judge rules it binding, or when he makes it hang on his death (a bequest-waqf).tap to reveal
What is Abū Yūsuf's view on the end of ownership?
وقال أبو يوسف: يزول الملك بمجرد القول
Ownership leaves as soon as the founder says it; no judge or handover needed.tap to reveal
What does Muḥammad require?
وقال محمد: لا يزول الملك حتى يجعل للوقف وليا ويسلمه إليه
Ownership leaves only after he appoints a custodian (walī) and hands the property over.tap to reveal

2 · Re-teach

Book of Usurpation (Ghaṣb)

Ghaṣb is taking another's property by force without right. The book states the core duty to return the thing itself, the liability for loss (its like for fungibles, its value for non-fungibles), the Imām's distinction that real estate is not 'taken' in the same way, and then a long chain of transformation cases: slaughtering, tearing, altering a thing until its name changes (ownership passes to the usurper but he may not benefit until paying), building on a plank, planting on land, dyeing cloth. It ends with the usurper's trust-status over offspring and fruit, benefits, and liquor of dhimmīs.
What does a usurper owe if the item perishes?
ومن غصب شيئا مما له مثل فهلك في يده فعليه ضمان مثله وإن كان مما لا مثل له فعليه قيمته يوم الغصب
The like for fungibles; the value on the day of ghaṣb for non-fungibles.tap to reveal
What is the first duty of the usurper?
وعلى الغاصب رد العين المغصوبة فإن ادعى هلاكها حبسه الحاكم حتى يعلم أنها لو كانت باقية لأظهرها ثم قضى عليه ببدلها
Return the item itself; if he claims it perished the judge jails him until it is clear he would have shown it, then orders the substitute.tap to reveal
Is real estate liable if it perishes in the usurper's hand?
وإذا غصب عقارا فهلك في يده لم يضمنه عند أبي حنيفة وأبي يوسف وقال محمد: يضمنه كهدمه
Per Abū Ḥanīfa and Abū Yūsuf, no ḍamān for mere loss (e.g. flood); Muḥammad says he is liable.tap to reveal

Book of Deposits (Wadīʿa)

A deposit is a trust: if it perishes without fault, the custodian owes nothing. The chapter lists what makes him liable: entrusting to strangers, mixing it with his property, using it, denying it on demand. It lists exceptions (fire, drowning), the power to travel with it, and splitting rules for two depositors or two custodians, then the rule of following the owner's restrictions on place of keeping.
Is a custodian liable if the deposit perishes?
الوديعة: أمانة في يد المودع إذا هلكت لم يضمنها
No, unless he transgressed.tap to reveal
Whom may the custodian have look after it?
وللمودع أن يحفظها بنفسه وبمن في عياله فإن حفظها بغيرهم أو أودعها ضمن
Himself and his dependants; handing it to anyone else makes him liable.tap to reveal
Mixing the deposit with his own property.
وإن خلطها المودع بماله حتى لا تتميز ضمنها
He is liable if they can no longer be separated.tap to reveal

Book of Loan for Use (ʿĀriya)

ʿĀriya is giving the use of an item without payment. It is a trust (no liability for non-negligent loss), revocable at will, with limits on what the borrower may do: no renting out, no pledging, lending on only if use does not vary with the user. Money and measured goods lent are qard (loan for consumption). The chapter closes with building on borrowed land and who pays return costs.
What is ʿāriya?
العارية: جائزة وهي: تمليك المنافع بغير عوض
It is permissible; it is giving ownership of benefits without compensation.tap to reveal
May the lender take it back?
وللمعير أن يرجع في العارية متى شاء
Yes, whenever he wishes.tap to reveal
Is the borrower liable?
والعارية أمانة: إن هلكت من غير تعد لم يضمن شيئا
No unless he is negligent or transgresses.tap to reveal

Book of the Foundling (Laqīṭ)

The foundling is a free Muslim child found abandoned. The state (bayt al-māl) supports him, the finder has priority in keeping him, lineage is established by a claim, and religion follows the place of finding. The finder is a custodian, not a guardian for marriage or the child's property.
What is the foundling's status and who supports him?
اللقيط: حر مسلم ونفقته من بيت المال
Free, Muslim; supported by the public treasury.tap to reveal

Chapter of Lost Property (Luqaṭa)

Lost property is a trust with the finder, who must announce it for a period set by its value, then give it to charity while keeping the owner's right to approve the charity or claim compensation. The chapter also treats strays, spending on them, and who may benefit from the item.
What is the finder's status and how long must he announce?
اللقطة: أمانة إذا أشهد الملتقط أنه يأخذها ليحفظها ويردها على صاحبها فإن كانت أقل من عشرة دراهم عرفها أياما وإن كانت عشرة فصاعدا عرفها حولا
Trust if he declares before witnesses; under ten dirhams a few days, ten and above a year.tap to reveal
After announcing, what?
فإن جاء صاحبها وإلا تصدق بها فإن جاء صاحبها فهو بالخيار: إن شاء أمضى الصدقة وإن شاء ضمن الملتقط
He gives it as charity; if the owner appears he approves it (reward is his) or takes compensation from the finder.tap to reveal
Who may benefit?
ولا يتصدق باللقطة على غني وإن كان الملتقط غنيا لم يجز له أن ينتفع بها وإن كان فقيرا فلا بأس أن ينتفع بها
No charity to the rich; a rich finder may not use it; a poor one may.tap to reveal

Book of the Hermaphrodite (Khunthā)

The khunthā has both male and female organs. Gender is determined by urination, then by signs at maturity. If undecided he is a khunthā mushkil, treated with caution in prayer, circumcision and inheritance. The three Imāms differ on shares.
How to determine gender before puberty?
إذا كان للمولود فرج وذكر فهو خنثى فإن كان يبول من الذكر فهو غلام وإن كان يبول من الفرج فهو أنثى
By the organ he urinates from.tap to reveal
Share in inheritance per Abū Ḥanīfa.
وإذا مات أبوه وخلف ابنا وخنثى فالمال بينهما عند أبي حنيفة على ثلاثة أسهم: للابن سهمان وللخنثى سهم
Three shares: son 2, khunthā 1.tap to reveal

Book of the Missing Person (Mafqūd)

The missing person is presumed alive in his own affairs (his property is kept, his wife stays his wife) but presumed dead as to taking from others (he does not inherit while missing). After the limit the judge declares him dead.
What happens to his property?
إذا غاب الرجل ولم يعرف له موضع ولا يعلم أحي هو أم ميت نصب القاضي من يحفظ ماله ويقوم عليه ويستوفي حقوقه وينفق على زوجته وأولاده من ماله
The judge appoints a manager who guards the property, collects dues and spends on his wife and children.tap to reveal
Is his wife separated?
ولا يفرق بينه وبين امرأته
No.tap to reveal
When is he declared dead?
فإذا تم له مائة وعشرون سنة من يوم ولد حكمنا بموته واعتدت امرأته وقسم ماله بين ورثته الموجودين في ذلك الوقت
At 120 years from birth; wife observes ʿidda; estate goes to the heirs alive at that time.tap to reveal

Book of the Runaway Slave (Ibāq)

The returner of a runaway slave earns a fixed reward depending on distance. The chapter fixes forty dirhams for a three-day journey, proportional for less, and capped for a cheap slave, plus the finder's duty to declare intent.
What is the reward?
إذا أبق مملوك فرده رجل على مولاه من مسيرة ثلاثة أيام فصاعدا فله عليه الجعل أربعون درهما
Forty dirhams for three days' journey or more.tap to reveal

Book of Reviving Dead Land (Iḥyāʾ al-Mawāt)

Dead land (mawāt) is land with no use and no owner, far from habitation. Whoever revives it with the imām's permission owns it (Abū Ḥanīfa), or even without it (the two). The chapter fixes the limits of the village commons and well-perimeters.
What is mawāt?
وهو بعيد من القرية بحيث إذا وقف إنسان في أقصى العامر فصاح لم يسمع الصوت فيه فهو موات
Far enough that a shout from the farthest inhabited edge cannot be heard there.tap to reveal
Who owns revived land?
من أحياه بإذن الإمام ملكه وإن أحياه بغير إذنه لم يملكه عند أبي حنيفة وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد: يملكه
Abū Ḥanīfa: ownership needs the imām's permission. The two: no permit needed.tap to reveal
Well perimeters.
ومن حفر بئرا في برية فله حريمها فإن كانت البئر للعطن فحريمها أربعون ذراعا وإن كانت للناضح فستون ذراعا وإن كانت عينا فحريمها ثلاثمائة ذراع
40 cubits for a camel-watering well, 60 for an irrigation well, 300 for a spring.tap to reveal

Book of the Authorised Slave (Maʾdhūn)

A slave authorised to trade acts as a trader within his scope; his debts attach to his person (value) and the master may free him by paying. The chapter also covers revoking the permission, transactions with the master, and the child permitted to trade.
Scope of general permission.
إذا أذن المولى لعبده في التجارة إذنا عاما جاز تصرفه في سائر التجارات
He may trade in all trades.tap to reveal
Debts.
وديونه متعلقة برقبته: يباع للغرماء إلا أن يفديه المولى ويقسم ثمنه بينهم بالحصص
Debts attach to his person; sold for creditors unless the master ransoms; price split pro rata.tap to reveal
How is permission revoked?
وإن حجر عليه لم يصر محجورا عليه حتى يظهر الحجر بين أهل سوقه فإن مات المولى أو جن أو لحق بدار الحرب مرتدا صار المأذون محجورا عليه
Revocation needs publicity among market people; the master's death, madness or apostasy-flight revokes.tap to reveal

Book of Sharecropping and Orchard Partnership

Muzāraʿa is farming land for a share of the crop; musāqāh is tending trees for a share of fruit. Abū Ḥanīfa holds both invalid (a share of unknown yield for work); his two students hold both valid with conditions (known term, undivided share, valid allocation of land, seed, work, oxen). Fatwā follows the two.
Is muzāraʿa valid?
المزارعة بالثلث والربع باطلة وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد: جائزة
Abū Ḥanīfa: invalid; his two students: valid.tap to reveal
Is a term needed?
ولا تصح المزارعة إلا على مدة معلومة
Yes, a known term.tap to reveal
What must the share be?
ومن شرائطها: أن يكون الخارج مشاعا بينهما فإن شرطا لأحدهما قفزانا مسماة فهي باطلة
An undivided share of the produce; a fixed measure for one party invalidates.tap to reveal

Nikāḥ I: The Contract and Witnesses

Nikāḥ is concluded by offer and acceptance in two past-tense words, or one past and one future. It needs two witnesses (two free, sane, adult Muslims, or a man and two women); their uprightness is not required. A Muslim may marry a Kitābiyya before dhimmī witnesses (Abū Ḥanīfa and Abū Yūsuf; Muḥammad disagrees). The wording of the contract includes words of tamlīk (ownership transfer) like hiba and ṣadaqa but not ijāra. Marriage during iḥrām is allowed in the Ḥanafī school, while mutʿa and temporary marriage are void.
How is nikāḥ concluded?
النكاح ينعقد بالإيجاب والقبول بلفظين يعبر بهما عن الماضي أو يعبر بأحدهما عن الماضي وبالآخر عن المستقبل مثل أن يقول: زوجتي فيقول زوجتك

ولا يعقد نكاح المسلمين إلا بحضور شاهدين حرين بالغين عاقلين مسلمين أو رجل وامرأتين عدولا كانوا غيرا عدول أو محدودين في قذف
Two free, adult, sane Muslims, or a man and two women; whether upright or not.tap to reveal

Nikāḥ II: Prohibited Women

The text lists the lasting prohibitions (blood, marriage ties and fosterage), then the prohibition of combining certain pairs, prohibitions from fornication and from a woman in ʿidda, and finally the lawful and unlawful religions of wives (Kitābiyyāt yes; Majūsiyyāt and idolaters no; Ṣābiʾa depending on belief).

Nikāḥ III: Guardianship and Equality (Kafāʾa)

Abū Ḥanīfa permits a free adult sane woman to marry herself; the two require a walī. No coercion for an adult virgin; silence counts as consent; the previously married must speak. The minor can be married by the guardian with a right of option after puberty unless it is father or grandfather. Kafāʾa is a right of the guardians; also rules on absence and dowry-reduction.
May an adult woman conclude her own marriage?
وينعقد نكاح المرأة الحرة البالغة العاقلة برضاها وإن لم يعقد عليها ولي عند أبي حنيفة بكر كانت أو ثيبا وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد: لا ينعقد إلا بولي
Abū Ḥanīfa: yes, virgin or not; the two: needs a walī.tap to reveal

Nikāḥ IV: The Dowry (Mahr)

The mahr is a right of the wife, not a pillar: the marriage is valid without naming it. Minimum ten dirhams. Half if divorce before consummation or khalwa; full on consummation or death. Mahr al-mithl applies when none named; mutʿa (three garments) if divorce before consummation. The rules of valid privacy (khalwa), conditions, defective naming, and comparison for mahr al-mithl are covered.
Is naming the dowry required?
ويصح النكاح إذا سمى فيه مهرا ويصح وإن لم يسم فيه مهرا
Valid either way.tap to reveal
Minimum?
وأقل المهر عشرة دراهم فإن سمى أقل من عشرة فلها العشرة
Ten dirhams; if less is named she gets ten.tap to reveal
Divorce before consummation.
ومن سمى مهرا عشرة فما زاد فعليه المسمى إن دخل بها أو مات عنها وإن طلقها قبل الدخول والخلوة فلها نصف المسمى
Full if consummated or death; half if divorced before consummation and khalwa.tap to reveal

Nikāḥ V: Slave Women, Number of Wives, and Options of Annulment

Marrying a slave woman is allowed only if no free wife is present; a free man may have four wives, a slave two. A freed slave woman gets an option. Combining a lawful and unlawful woman in one contract voids only the unlawful. The chapter then covers defects: the husband's impotence (a year's delay), insanity (annulment per Muḥammad), and the effect.
How many wives?
وللحر أن يتزوج أربعا من الحرائر والإماء وليس له أن يتزوج أكثر من ذلك
Free man: four.tap to reveal
Divorcing one of the four.
أن يتزوج رابعة حتى تنقضي عدتها
After an irrevocable divorce of one, he may not marry another until her ʿidda ends.tap to reveal
Defects in spouses.
وإن كان بالزوجة عيب فلا خيار لزوجها وإذا كان بالزوج جنون أو جذام أو برص فلا خيار للمرأة عند أبي حنيفة وأبي يوسف وقال محمد: لها الخيار
No option for either on those defects per Abū Ḥanīfa and Abū Yūsuf; Muḥammad gives her an option.tap to reveal

Nikāḥ VI: Conversion and Apostasy

The chapter explains what happens when a spouse converts or apostatises: the judge offers Islam to the other spouse; refusal leads to separation, a divorce when the husband refuses (Abū Ḥanīfa, Muḥammad) and not a divorce when the wife refuses. Residence in the abode of war, ʿidda, mahr, children's religion, and disbelievers' marriages that are accepted if they accepted Islam.
Wife converts, husband refuses.
عرض عليه القاضي الإسلام فإن أسلم فهي امرأته وإن أبى عن الإسلام فرق بينهما وكان ذلك طلاقا بائنا عند أبي حنيفة ومحمد
Judge offers Islam; if he refuses, separate; a bāʾin divorce per Abū Ḥanīfa and Muḥammad.tap to reveal
Husband converts, wife Majūsiyya.
فإن أسلم الزوج وتحته مجوسية عرض عليها الإسلام فإن أسلمت فهي امرأته وإن أبت فرق القاضي بينهما ولم تكن هذه الفرقة طلاقا
Offer Islam; refusal leads to separation which is not a divorce.tap to reveal
Husband of a Kitābiyya converts.

Nikāḥ VII: Equal Time Between Wives (Qasm)

A man with several wives must treat them equally in nights. The free wife has two shares to the slave wife's one. No claim on a journey; he may choose or draw lots; wives may give up their turn and retract.
Equal nights?
