Kitāb al-Jināyāt — kinds of killing

Qudūrī lists five kinds of killing and their consequences. Hidāya adds the proofs (Qurʾān 4:93, 2:178, 4:92, the ḥadīths 'al-ʿamd qawd' and 'qatīl khaṭaʾ al-ʿamd'), the Shāfiʿī dispute over what the intentional-killing right is (qiṣāṣ only, or qiṣāṣ-or-diya), the kaffāra dispute, and the Imām vs Ṣāḥibayn dispute on what counts as 'weapon' — this last dispute defines shibh al-ʿamd. It also explains why qatl bi-sabab carries diya but no kaffāra and no loss of inheritance.
What is the textual proof that ʿamd carries sin, qawd, and loss of inheritance?
وقد نطق به غير واحد من السنة، وعليه انعقد إجماع الأمة
Sin from Q 4:93 (plus sunna and ijmāʿ); qawd from Q 2:178 as restricted by 'al-ʿamd qawd'; loss of inheritance from 'lā mīrāth li-qātil'.tap to reveal
Is the guardian's right in ʿamd specifically qiṣāṣ, or may he insist on diya?
وليس للولي أخذ الدية إلا برضا القاتل وهو أحد قولي الشافعي
Ḥanafī: only qiṣāṣ is due; the guardian may take diya only with the killer's consent (or settle). Shāfiʿī, one report: qiṣāṣ is specific but the guardian may switch to money without consent to save the killer's life; second report: one of the two, undetermined, fixed by the guardian's choice.tap to reveal
Is kaffāra due for intentional killing?
ولنا أنه كبيرة محضة، وفي الكفارة معنى العبادة فلا تناط بمثلها
No kaffāra for ʿamd. Shāfiʿī: due, because the need for expiation is greater in ʿamd than in khaṭaʾ (a fortiori).tap to reveal

What necessitates qiṣāṣ and what does not

Qudūrī gives the conditions of qiṣāṣ (a person protected permanently, killed intentionally) and its exceptions. Hidāya's contribution is the khilāf and the proofs: free for slave and Muslim for dhimmī (Shāfiʿī denies), the father-son exception (Mālik: if he slaughters), the manner of execution (sword only, vs. like-for-like), the mukātab's heirs, who may execute for the incompetent and the minor, killing with a heavy blunt object and drowning (the Imām vs. Ṣāḥibayn and al-Shāfiʿī), and the 'drawing the sword' faṣl on self-defence. The reasoning principle: qiṣāṣ rests on equality in ʿiṣma (protection), not on rank or worth.
Is a free man killed for killing a slave?
ولنا أن القصاص يعتمد المساواة في العصمة وهي بالدين وبالدار ويستويان فيهما
Yes, in the Ḥanafī school. Shāfiʿī: no, citing 'al-ḥurr bil-ḥurr wal-ʿabd bil-ʿabd' (2:178) and the absence of equality between owner and owned. Ḥanafī: the verse mentions without negating the rest; qiṣāṣ rests on equal ʿiṣma, which comes from religion and abode, and in both the two are equal.tap to reveal
Is a Muslim killed for killing a dhimmī?
أن النبي ﷺ قتل مسلما بذمي
Yes (Ḥanafī). Shāfiʿī: no, citing 'lā yuqtal muʾmin bi-kāfir' and the lack of equality at the time of crime, and because disbelief permits (a doubt). Ḥanafī reply: equality of ʿiṣma holds by taklīf and abode; the only disbelief that permits is that of the ḥarbī, and the ḥadīth means the ḥarbī, shown by its sequel 'wa lā dhū ʿahd fī ʿahdih'.tap to reveal
Why is a father not killed for his son? Does Mālik differ?
ولأنه سبب لإحيائه، فمن المحال أن يستحق له إفناؤه
Proof: 'lā yuqād al-wālid bi-waladih' and reason: the father is the cause of the son's existence, so it is impossible that destroying him be owed to him. Mālik: he is killed if he slaughtered him (dhabḥan); the ḥadīth's absolute wording is proof against him. The grandfather and mother and grandmother, any degree, are like the father. The son is killed for his father.tap to reveal

Qiṣāṣ below the person; settlement; multiple offenders; sirāya

Qudūrī gives the limits of qiṣāṣ in limbs. Hidāya supplies the criterion (mumāthala that can be guaranteed: 'al-jurūḥ qiṣāṣ'), the reason limbs follow property rather than lives (no qiṣāṣ between man/woman, free/slave: Shāfiʿī differs), and then the long set of far's: settlement (ṣulḥ) on money and the pardon of one heir (Mālik/Shāfiʿī: spouses do not inherit), a group killing one (ʿUmar: 'if the people of Sanʿāʾ conspired I would kill them'), two cutters of a hand (Shāfiʿī: both hands), the sequence of cut then killing (aggregation of wounds), the effect of pardon on sirāya (spread of a wound to death), and who bears liability when a qiṣāṣ cut spreads to death (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn).
What is the criterion for qiṣāṣ in a limb?
فكل ما أمكن رعايتها فيه يجب فيه القصاص وما لا فلا
Qiṣāṣ wherever equivalence can be guaranteed (cut at a joint, tooth filed or pulled, eye by mirror and wet cotton), none where not (eye plucked, bone but the tooth, forearm mid-shaft, jāʾifa). Size of hand does not matter, since use is the same.tap to reveal
Is there qiṣāṣ for a limb between man/woman or free/slave?
ولنا أن الأطراف يسلك بها مسلك الأموال فينعدم التماثل بالتفاوت في القيمة
No (Ḥanafī). Shāfiʿī: yes in all of these, except a free man cutting a slave's limb, treating limbs like lives. Ḥanafī: limbs follow property, so the difference in value (known with certainty by the Lawgiver's valuation) destroys equivalence; unlike lives, where the loss is departure of the soul without degrees. Between Muslim and kāfir, qiṣāṣ applies, since arsh is equal.tap to reveal
May the family settle for money, and how much?
والقليل والكثير فيه سواء لأنه ليس فيه نص مقدر فيفوض إلى اصطلاحهما
Yes, by agreement, less or more, since no fixed text exists, as in khulʿ; it is due immediately, being a contractual debt, unlike diya which is due in instalments (not by contract). Proof: 'fa-man ʿufiya lahu…' (2:178, said to be about ṣulḥ).tap to reveal

Testimony in killing; time of the act vs time of the result

Two bābs that Qudūrī does not map. First: evidence of killing when one heir is absent (does the present heir's proof bind the absent one?), contradictory witnesses (a killing cannot be repeated), and witnesses who say 'we do not know with what he killed'. Second: which moment counts, the act or the result? Abū Ḥanīfa: the moment of the act (shot) governs, so a Muslim shot who apostatises before the arrow lands still gets diya; the same principle drives ṣayd, ḥajj and rajm rulings.
One of two sons is present and proves the killing; must the absent son prove it again?
وله أن القصاص طريقه الخلافة دون الوراثة؛ ألا ترى أن ملك القصاص يثبت بعد الموت
AH: yes, he repeats the proof. Ṣāḥibayn: no. In khaṭaʾ (diya) none repeats, by agreement. Ṣāḥibayn: qiṣāṣ is inherited like a debt, as it is a replacement for the person (so if turned to money it is the dead man's, and pardon before death waives it), so one heir stands for the others. AH: it is succession, not inheritance, since the right is created after death and the dead man is not capable; unlike debt and diya, where he is capable of ownership (like a net that catches game after his death). So no heir stands for another.tap to reveal
A man shoots a Muslim who then apostatises before the arrow lands. Liability?
وله أن الضمان يجب بفعله وهو الرمي إذ لا فعل منه بعد فتعتبر حالة الرمي
AH: diya on the shooter. Ṣāḥibayn: nothing, since apostasy wiped his worth and works like a discharge after the wound. AH: liability comes from the act (the shot), and there is no act after it, so the time of the shot governs, when the target was worth protecting (as for ḥill and kaffāra, taken at the shot). Qiṣāṣ falls by doubt; diya remains. The reverse (shot an apostate who then converted, or a ḥarbī who converted): nothing, by all.tap to reveal
Which moment counts for rajm, ṣayd, and the muḥrim?
لأن المعتبر حال الرمي في حق الحل والحرمة إذ الرمي هو الذكاة
The moment of the shot: stoning of a man whose witness retracts after the stone is thrown: nothing. Magian shoots game then converts: not eaten; Muslim shoots then turns Magian: eaten. Muḥrim shoots then leaves iḥrām: penalty (jazāʾ); a non-muḥrim who shoots then enters iḥrām: nothing.tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Diyāt — kaffāra and the amount of diya

Qudūrī states the diya of shibh al-ʿamd and khaṭaʾ. Hidāya adds: the kaffāra for killing (freeing a believing slave, then two months' fasting, no feeding; a nursing child of a Muslim parent is valid, a foetus not); the khilāf on the taghlīẓ (AH/AY quarters; Muḥammad and Shāfiʿī thirds with pregnant camels); the khaṭaʾ diya in fifths (Ibn Masʿūd); the money equivalents (1000 dīnār / 10,000 dirhams, Shāfiʿī 12,000); whether cattle, sheep, garments are fixed forms (AH: no); the woman's diya (half) and the dhimmī's (equal: vs Shāfiʿī and Mālik).
What are the forms of kaffāra for killing, and is feeding allowed?
ولا يجزئ فيه الإطعام" لأنه لم يرد به نص والمقادير تعرف بالتوقيف
A believing slave, otherwise two consecutive months of fasting by the text; feeding is not accepted, since quantities are known only by revelation, and the verse stated what was due with the particle 'fa'. A nursing child with one Muslim parent is valid (Muslim by that parent; limbs sound); a foetus is not.tap to reveal
How is the diya of shibh al-ʿamd divided in camels?
ولهما قوله ﵊: "في نفس المؤمن مائة من الإبل" وما روياه غير ثابت لاختلاف الصحابة ﵃
AH/AY: quarters (25 bint makhāḍ, 25 bint labūn, 25 ḥiqqa, 25 jadhaʿa). Muḥammad and Shāfiʿī: thirds (30 jadhaʿa, 30 ḥiqqa, 40 thaniyya all pregnant), by the ḥadīth 'qatīl al-sawṭ wal-ʿaṣā… forty of them with young in their bellies', and ʿUmar's thirty ḥiqqa and thirty jadhaʿa. AH/AY: 'in the life of a believer a hundred camels'; their ḥadīth is not established, as the Companions differed, and Ibn Masʿūd's quarters are like marfūʿ.tap to reveal
How is the khaṭaʾ diya divided in camels?
أن النبي ﷺ قضى في قتيل قتل خطأ أخماسا
Fifths: 20 bint makhāḍ, 20 bint labūn, 20 ibn makhāḍ, 20 ḥiqqa, 20 jadhaʿa (Ibn Masʿūd, in line with the Prophet's judgment); lighter, hence fitting for one who erred. Shāfiʿī replaces ibn makhāḍ with ibn labūn; the narrations answer him.tap to reveal

Diya for limbs and the ten head wounds (shijāj)

Qudūrī lists the organs carrying a full diya. Hidāya supplies the governing principle (a full diya when a whole kind of benefit or a sought-after beauty is destroyed completely, since it is a near-killing), with the ḥadīths (Ibn al-Musayyib; the letter to ʿAmr b. Ḥazm), and then the far's: hair and beard (Mālik/Shāfiʿī: only ḥukūma), the Kawsaj, eyelashes, a paralysed limb, back injuries. In shijāj: the ten names, qiṣāṣ only in the mūḍiḥa (and Muḥammad's earlier wounds), the fixed amounts with the ḥadīths, ʿAbū Bakr's ruling on a penetrating jāʾifa, and the definition of ḥukūmat ʿadl (Ṭaḥāwī vs Karkhī).
What is the principle for which organs carry a full diya?
والأصل في الأطراف أنه إذا فوت جنس منفعة على الكمال أو أزال جمالا مقصودا في الآدمي على الكمال يجب كل الدية
When a whole kind of benefit is lost completely, or a sought-after beauty is removed completely, the full diya is due, as it is a partial killing treated as total in honour of the human being; model: the Prophet's judgment of full diya for tongue and nose.tap to reveal
What is due for loss of intellect, hearing, sight, smell, taste?
وقد روي: أن عمر ﵁ قضى بأربع ديات في ضربة واحدة
A full diya each, as each is a sought benefit; ʿUmar judged four diyas for one blow that removed intellect, speech, hearing and sight.tap to reveal
Is a full diya due for shaving a beard or head hair that does not grow back?
ولنا أن اللحية في وقتها جمال وفي حلقها تفويته على الكمال فتجب الدية
Yes. Mālik and Shāfiʿī: only ḥukūmat ʿadl (hair is an addition, shaved off in places, like chest hair; for a slave, the loss of value). Ḥanafī: the beard at its time is beauty, like the standing ears; head hair is beauty (a bald-born person covers it), unlike chest and shin hair. For the slave's beard, AH: his full value (use matters, not beauty).tap to reveal

Hand and finger far's; aggregation; ʿāqila vs own money

Hidāya works out the detailed far's of limb compensation: fingers with palm and forearm (AH: the fingers are the root, the palm follows; Ṣāḥibayn: the greater of the two), extra finger and doubtful organs of a child (Shāfiʿī: full diya), the entering of the mūḍiḥa into the diya for intellect (Zufar: no), the khilāf over qiṣāṣ for a mūḍiḥa that spreads to loss of sight, regrowth of teeth, waiting a year for a wound (Shāfiʿī: immediate), and who pays: the ʿāqila never bears intentional killing, admission or settlement. The child/insane person's ʿamd is khaṭaʾ (Shāfiʿī: ʿamd).
The palm is cut with one or two fingers on it?
وله أن الأصابع أصل والكف تابع حقيقة وشرعا، لأن البطش يقوم بها
AH: arsh of the fingers only (a tenth for one, a fifth for two), nothing for the palm. Ṣāḥibayn: the greater of the palm's arsh and the fingers', the lesser enters the greater. AH: fingers are the root; precedence by nature and ruling over precedence by amount. If three fingers: arsh of fingers only by all.tap to reveal
A child's eye, member or tongue whose soundness is unknown?
ولنا أن المقصود من هذه الأعضاء المنفعة، فإن لم يعلم صحتها لا يجب الأرش الكامل بالشك
Ḥukūmat ʿadl. Shāfiʿī: full diya, as health is usual (like the nose tip or ear). Ḥanafī: benefit is the aim, and a doubtful benefit does not oblige the full amount; appearance alone cannot bind. Soundness is shown by cry (istihlāl does not suffice), speech for the tongue, movement for the member, sight-reaction for the eye. Extra finger and extra tooth: ḥukūma.tap to reveal
A mūḍiḥa causes loss of intellect or hair; is the mūḍiḥa arsh added?
لأن بفوات العقل تبطل منفعة جميع الأعضاء فصار كما إذا أوضحه فمات
It enters the diya (intellect, scalp hair); Zufar: no, being injuries below the person that do not overlap. For hearing, sight or speech: the mūḍiḥa arsh is added to the diya (AH and AY); AY, another report: it enters hearing and speech but not sight (hidden benefits like the intellect; sight is visible). Ḥanafī: reason is the benefit of all organs.tap to reveal

The foetus (janīn) and ghurra

Qudūrī gives the ghurra for a foetus lost by a blow. Hidāya proves it (the ḥadīth 'fī al-janīn ghurra'), explains why it is by istiḥsān against qiyās, fixes it at 500 dirhams (Mālik/Shāfiʿī: 600), puts it on the ʿāqila (Mālik: own money) in one year (Shāfiʿī: three), and treats the mother-and-child death scenarios, the father who strikes his wife (no inheritance), the slave woman's foetus, and kaffāra (Shāfiʿī: due).
What is the basis for the ghurra and its amount?
فتركنا القياس بالأثر، وهو حجة على من قدرها بستمائة نحو مالك والشافعي
Analogy: nothing, since life is not certain; the ḥadīth: 'in the foetus a ghurra, a slave or slave woman worth 500' (or 500 dirhams) overrides it, a proof against Mālik and Shāfiʿī who fix 600. Male: half a tenth of a man's diya; female: a tenth of a woman's: both 500.tap to reveal
Who pays the ghurra and when?
ولنا ما روي عن محمد بن الحسن ﵀ أنه قال: "بلغنا أن رسول الله ﵊ جعله على العاقلة في سنة"

What a man builds on the road; the leaning wall

Qudūrī mentions digging a well, placing objects on the road, riders and the leaning wall in a few lines. Hidāya builds the doctrine: the public road is a shared right, so anyone may demand removal of an encroachment (dukkān, jāʾiz, mīzāb); a dead-end lane belongs to its people; liability follows 'taʿaddī' (transgression) through causation (tasabbub), and an intervening free act of another cuts the attribution. Special rulings for hirers (workmen vs owner), the mosque of a tribe (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn), and the leaning wall: notice and demand needed, by istiḥsān, and what happens in co-owned walls (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn). Death by hunger in a pit (AH/AY/Muḥammad).
A gutter or projection falls and kills; and if only part is in the wall?
وإن أصابه ما كان خارجا من الحائط فالضمان على الذي وضعه فيه
Diya on his ʿāqila (he is a cause and transgressed by occupying road air); no kaffāra, no loss of inheritance. If the part in the wall hits: nothing (his own property). If both hit: half diya, half void (as lion + man); if unknown: half. A sale of the house after fixing the projection: the seller remains liable (his act is not undone by the sale).tap to reveal
If someone digs in the road: person and animal?
لأنه متعد فيه فيضمن ما يتولد منه، غير أن العاقلة تتحمل النفس دون المال
Diya on the ʿāqila; for an animal: from his own money (the ʿāqila do not bear property). If he digs on the sultan's order: no liability; in his own land or his own forecourt: none, but where the forecourt is shared (dead-end lane) he is liable.tap to reveal
If the person fell in the pit but died of hunger or suffocation?
وقال محمد: هو ضامن في الوجوه كلها، لأنه إنما حدث بسبب الوقوع
AH: no liability (death from a cause in himself). AY: hunger no, suffocation yes (nothing causes it but the fall; hunger is not specific to a pit). Muḥammad: liable in all (it arose from falling; otherwise food would be near).tap to reveal

Offences by and against animals

Qudūrī gives the rider's liability in a line. Hidāya states the governing principle: passage on the road is permitted on condition of safety only where avoidance is possible (trampling yes; kick of foot or tail no, unless he stopped). It then sorts rider, driver (sāʾiq) and leader (qāʾid), the camel train, the sending of an animal, the pricking (nakhs) of an animal, the collision of two riders (Zufar/Shāfiʿī: half) and the compensation for the eye of an animal (quarter of the value, vs Shāfiʿī: loss).
What is the principle behind the rider's liability?
والأصل أن المرور في طريق المسلمين مباح مقيد بشرط السلامة لأنه يتصرف في حقه من وجه وفي حق غيره من وجه
Passage is permitted on condition of safety, but only where avoidance is possible: treading, biting and butting are avoidable (liable); a kick of foot or tail while moving is not. If he stops the animal on the road he is liable for the kick as well, since stopping is avoidable. Pebbles or dust: none; a big stone: liable. Dung and urine in motion: none; stopped for other reasons: liable.tap to reveal
Are the driver and leader liable for the kick?
وقال الشافعي: يضمنون النفحة كلهم لأن فعلها مضاف إليهم، والحجة عليه ما ذكرناه، وقوله ﵊: "الرجل جبار"
Qudūrī's text: driver liable for hand and foot, leader for hand only. Most mashāyikh: neither for the kick even if the driver sees it (nothing to hold the leg; biting can be checked by the bridle): the sounder. Shāfiʿī: all liable for the kick; reply: 'al-rijl jubār', the kick of the foot. All that binds the rider binds driver and leader (they cause it by drawing the animal near).tap to reveal
Two riders collide and both die.
ولنا أن الموت يضاف إلى فعل صاحبه لأن فعله في نفسه مباح وهو المشي في الطريق
Each's ʿāqila owes the full diya of the other. Zufar and Shāfiʿī (from ʿAlī): half the other's diya, as each died by his own act and his companion's (half void). Ḥanafī: his own act is permitted (walking), so cannot be a ground for ascription; the other's permitted act still causes liability, like a sleeper rolling; another report from ʿAlī: full diya. Two slaves: hadr; one free, one slave: slave's value to the free man's heirs.tap to reveal

Offences by and against a slave

Qudūrī says a slave's mistaken offence gives the master the choice of handing him over or ransoming him. Hidāya states why (Shāfiʿī: sale of the slave, debt on him; Ḥanafī: the offence is kept off the offender since he is excused, falls on the ʿāqila, and the master is his ʿāqila), the structure (original duty is dafʿ, fidāʾ is a substitute), multiple offences and heirs, and the master who freed him, and then offences against a slave: the cap of the value below the free man's diya (AH/Muḥammad: 10,000 less ten; AY/Shāfiʿī: whole value) and the doubts that block qiṣāṣ when a wounded slave is freed (AH/AY vs Muḥammad).
Why does the master choose between handing over and ransoming, rather than the slave being sold?
ولنا أن الأصل في الجناية على الآدمي حالة الخطإ أن تتباعد عن الجاني تحرزا عن استئصاله والإجحاف به
Shāfiʿī: the offence lies on the slave's neck and he is sold unless the master pays (practical effect: pursuit after emancipation). Reasoning: the offender owes; the ʿāqila bear for kin and a slave has none; so it is a debt on his person. Ḥanafī: in the mistaken offence on a person, liability is pushed off the offender (an excused person); the master is his ʿāqila, as the slave seeks his support; so the master chooses. Dhimmīs have no ʿāqila, so it falls on them. Property offences go on the neck, ʿāqila bear no property.tap to reveal
What is due for killing another's slave by mistake: his full value or capped?
أوجبها مطلقا، وهي اسم للواجب بمقابلة الآدمية
AH/Muḥammad: his value, capped at 10,000 dirhams less ten (a slave woman: 5,000 less ten). AY and Shāfiʿī: value however large (as in ghaṣb, by agreement). AY/Shāfiʿī: the liability is for worth, paid to the master, who owns him only as property. AH/Muḥammad: the verse makes 'diya' absolute, the word for what is owed for personhood; the slave has two aspects (personhood, since he is addressed by law, and property), personhood the higher, so the lower is dropped; the free man's 10,000 is lowered to show his lower rank (ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās).tap to reveal
A slave's hand is cut, the master frees him, and he dies of it: qiṣāṣ?
وقال محمد: لا قصاص في ذلك، وعلى القاطع أرش اليد
AH/AY: if he has heirs besides the master, no qiṣāṣ (doubt as to who owns the right: the master at the wound, the heirs at death); else qiṣāṣ. Muḥammad: no qiṣāṣ in either: the cause differs (ownership vs walāʾ), and emancipation cuts the sirāya; the cutter pays the hand's arsh and the loss to the time of freeing, the rest voided. AH/AY: the master's right is certain; and freeing cuts sirāya only by uncertainty of right, which exists only in mistake (where the slave cannot own money), not intent.tap to reveal

Mudabbar and umm walad offences; usurped slave and child

Beyond Qudūrī, Hidāya treats the slave whose eyes are put out (AH: master chooses to hand over for the full value or keep without compensation; Ṣāḥibayn: keep and take the loss; Shāfiʿī: full value and keep him), the mudabbar and umm walad (liability for the lesser of value and arsh, one value only), and the interplay of ghaṣb and offences (the usurper's hand cutting sirāya; offences in the hands of two holders), the usurped free child, and the child or slave who destroys a deposit (AH/Muḥammad: not liable; AY/Shāfiʿī: liable).
Someone puts out both eyes of a slave; what are the master's rights?
وله أن المالية وإن كانت معتبرة في الذات فالآدمية غير مهدرة فيه
AH: hand over the slave and take his full value, or keep him with no compensation for the loss. Ṣāḥibayn: keep and take the loss, or hand him over and take the full value (as for a torn garment). Shāfiʿī: the full value and he keeps the body (the compensation is for the lost part, like one eye). AH: it is a near-destruction (a whole kind of benefit lost), and a value cannot be paid without taking the body, for equivalence; unlike the free man's eyes (not property) and the mudabbar (who cannot move owner).tap to reveal
What does the master owe for the offence of a mudabbar or umm walad?
ولأنه صار مانعا عن تسليمه في الجناية بالتدبير أو الاستيلاد من غير اختياره الفداء
The lesser of his value and the arsh (Abū ʿUbayda's judgment): he blocked the handover without choosing ransom; claimants have no right above the arsh, and the master did not block above the value. No choice between lesser and greater since the lesser would be chosen. Many offences give one value, shared pro rata, each by his value on the day of the offence on him; if freed afterwards, still one value. An admission of mistaken offence by the mudabbar is void.tap to reveal

Ḥanafī: not liable for ghaṣb but for destruction by causation: he moved him to a place of beasts or lightning and removed the guardian's protection; a condition is a cause when done wrongfully. Bāb al-Qasāma (the oath of fifty)

Qudūrī gives the procedure (fifty men of the quarter swear; diya on the quarter). Hidāya adds the great khilāf: Shāfiʿī and Mālik start with the oath of the guardians when there is lawth (indication), and Shāfiʿī gives diya (Mālik qawd) on that oath; the Ḥanafīs start with the oath of the defendants, as 'the oath is on the one who denies', and add diya to the oath. It then maps the tests of who is included: AH/Muḥammad exclude tenants and later purchasers (AY includes all), what counts as a body found (more than half), how animals, ships, mosques, roads, camps, prisons and desert are treated, and the effect of naming an accused.
Who swears first in qasāma, and does the oath alone give diya?
ولنا قوله ﵊: "البينة على المدعي واليمين على من أنكر"
Ḥanafī: the defendants swear and diya is due on them. Shāfiʿī: with lawth, the guardians swear fifty oaths and take diya from the defendant (intent or mistake); Mālik: qawd in intent (one report of Shāfiʿī); without lawth he is as ours but returns the oath to the guardians. Their proof: 'fifty of you will swear that they killed him', and the oath is on the side the apparent sign supports. Ḥanafī: 'the proof is on the claimant, the oath on the one who denies'; Saʿīd's report that the Prophet began with the Jews and put the diya on them; an oath defends but does not create a right, and it cannot even gain a trifle.tap to reveal
After they swear, is diya still due?
ولنا أن النبي ﵊ جمع بين الدية والقسامة في حديث ابن سهل
Yes, on the quarter. Shāfiʿī: no diya ('the Jews clear you by their oaths'; the oath only clears). Ḥanafī: the Prophet joined diya and oath in Ibn Sahl's and Ziyād's reports, and so did ʿUmar for Wādiʿa; 'clears you' means from qiṣāṣ and imprisonment. Diya is due for the apparent killing in their midst or their failure to protect. A refuser is jailed till he swears (the oath is due in itself).tap to reveal
What makes the finding a killing; what of parts of a body?
والأصل فيه أن الموجود الأول إن كان بحال لو وجد الباقي تجري فيه القسامة لا تجب فيه
A mark of wound, blow, strangling, blood from eye or ear (not mouth/anus/member). Body, more than half, or half with the head: qasāma and diya; half split lengthways, less than half with head, a hand, a leg, a head: nothing. Principle: if the first part found would allow qasāma when the rest is found, none is held for it. Foetus: full-formed with a mark of beating: yes; incomplete: none.tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Maʿāqil (the ʿāqila)

Qudūrī gives who the ʿāqila are and how diya is paid. Hidāya supplies the proofs and principles: the ʿāqila bear diya because a person's blood is not wasted and the erring is excused, and only those who failed to restrain him through their support (nuṣra) bear it; since ʿUmar's registers (dīwān) it is the dīwān people (Shāfiʿī: the clan, half a dīnār each); three years, from the date of judgment; the killer shares as one of them (Shāfiʿī: not); women and children do not; the threshold of half a tenth (arsh of the mūḍiḥa); no bearing for ʿamd, slave, ṣulḥ or admission (unless they confirm). Then the portability of liability when the killer's status changes: before judgment it moves, after judgment it does not.
Why do the ʿāqila bear the diya?
ولأن النفس محترمة لا وجه إلى الإهدار والخاطئ معذور
'Get up and pay it' (Ḥamal b. Mālik's ḥadīth): a person's life cannot be wasted; the erring is excused, and loading him with a big sum would ruin him and be a punishment. They are singled out because his strength is through his supporters, who failed to watch over him.tap to reveal
Is the ʿāqila the clan or the dīwān people?
ولنا قضية عمر ﵁ فإنه لما دون الدواوين جعل العقل على أهل الديوان
Dīwān people (from their stipends, three years). Shāfiʿī: the clan, as in the Prophet's time, no abrogation, and because it is a kindness (ṣila). Ḥanafī: ʿUmar did it before the Companions without objection; it is not abrogation but keeping the meaning: nuṣra was by kinship, alliance, patronage, now the register; if people support each other by trade, the trade people; by alliance, the allies.tap to reveal
How are instalments reckoned?
وإنما يعتبر مدة ثلاث سنين من وقت القضاء بالدية لأن الواجب الأصلي المثل والتحول إلى القيمة بالقضاء
From the judgment (the original due is the like, becoming money by judgment). A third each year: a third of the diya or less in one year; to two thirds in the second; the rest in the third. Ten men killing by mistake: a tenth each. The killer's own-money diya (father kills son) is also three years; Shāfiʿī: immediate.tap to reveal

Masāʾil Shattā — miscellany (the mute, the doubtful meat) and closing

The closing chapter of Hidāya gathers two cases: legal acts of the mute and the person whose tongue is temporarily tied (writing, signs; Shāfiʿī: valid in both), the three grades of writing, why signs are valid for rights of people but not ḥudūd, and the choice of meat when slaughtered and carrion sheep are mixed (investigation permitted if the slaughtered are more; Shāfiʿī: not in choice). The book ends with the prayer 'wa ilayhi al-marjiʿ wa-l-maʾāb'.
A mute person is read the document of his will; does his nod suffice?
والفرق لأصحابنا ﵏ أن الإشارة إنما تعتبر إذا صارت معهودة معلومة وذلك في الأخرس دون المعتقل لسانه
Valid for the mute (a nod, a writing). Not for one whose tongue is temporarily tied. Shāfiʿī: both (the excuse is inability, whether original or accidental, like wild and strayed domestic animals for slaughter). Ḥanafī: sign counts when it is known and customary, which occurs in the mute only (if it lasts and he has known signs, he is like the mute); the tongue-tied delayed his will; temporary is not like original; the abda (deer, wild) is by text.tap to reveal
What can a mute person do by writing or sign?
وإذا كان الأخرس يكتب كتابا أو يومئ إيماء يعرف به فإنه يجوز نكاحه وطلاقه وعتاقه وبيعه وشراؤه ويقتص له ومنه، ولا يحد ولا يحد له
Marriage, divorce, manumission, sale, purchase; qiṣāṣ for and against him; no ḥadd on or for him (ḥudūd are God's right and fall by doubt; he might have agreed with the slander; no explicit qadhf). Qiṣāṣ has an element of exchange so it may stand with a doubt, unlike pure ḥadd. Writing has three grades: clear and formal (like speech), clear but not formal (wall, leaves: needs intention), unclear (air, water: nothing).
