Kitāb al-Aymān I: kinds, wording, kaffāra

Qudūrī gives the three kinds of oath, the valid formulas and the kaffāra. Hidāya adds the evidence (the ghamūs hadith, Māʾida 89, the hadith on three things whose earnest is earnest) and above all the Shāfiʿī disputes: kaffāra for the ghamūs, expiating before breaking the oath, the order of fasting, and forbidding what is lawful. It also adds the Ḥanafī sub-disputes on 'wa-ḥaqqi llāh' and the fatwā-level ruling that 'everything lawful is forbidden to me' now effects talāq by custom.
Is there a kaffāra for the ghamūs oath?
وقال الشافعي ﵀ فيها الكفارة لأنها شرعت لرفع ذنب هتك حرمة اسم الله تعالى
Al-Shāfiʿī: kaffāra is due, because kaffāra exists to remove the sin of violating God's name, and that sin is complete by invoking God falsely. Ḥanafīs: only tawba and istighfār.tap to reveal
What is the hadith that the ghamūs is a sin?
من حلف كاذبا أدخله الله النار
Whoever swears falsely, God will enter him into the Fire — Hidāya's proof that the oath carries sin, not kaffāra.tap to reveal
What is the Qurʾānic basis for the kaffāra of the mun'aqida?
لا يُؤَاخِذُكُمُ اللَّهُ بِاللَّغْوِ فِي أَيْمَانِكُمْ وَلَكِنْ يُؤَاخِذُكُمْ بِمَا عَقَّدْتُمُ الْأَيْمَانَ
Māʾida 89: God takes you to task for what you bound by oath, i.e. the future oath. Same āya is the basis of laghw.tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Aymān II: applications of oaths (entering, eating, speaking, vows)

These bābs are Qudūrī's applications: what breaks an oath of entering, leaving, eating, speaking, freeing, walking to Makka. Hidāya mostly adds the reasoning behind why the Imām and his students split, and the key is always the same question: do oaths follow the literal meaning (ḥaqīqa mustaʿmala) or custom (ʿurf)? Abū Ḥanīfa gives priority to a used literal meaning; the Ṣāḥibayn follow current custom. Hidāya repeatedly marks such differences as ikhtilāf ʿaṣr wa-zamān, and says the fatwā follows custom. Another recurring axis is whether the oath requires that fulfilment be conceivable (taṣawwur al-birr).
Does an oath not to enter 'this house' bind after it is ruined?
لأن الدار اسم للعرصة عند العرب والعجم يقال دار عامرة ودار غامرة
A dār is the compound and not the building (a ruin is a dār); so entering the plot after collapse breaches 'this dār'. By contrast a bayt is a place for overnighting: once the roof and walls are gone it is no bayt, but with walls standing it stays.tap to reveal
If someone swears not to wear/ride/live in X while doing so, is he in breach by continuing a moment?
وقال زفر ﵀ يحنث لوجود الشرط وإن قل ولنا أن اليمين تعقد للبر فيستثنى منه زمان تحققه
Zufar: breach, as the condition occurred even briefly. Ḥanafīs: no breach if he stops at once, because an oath is for fulfilment and the time needed to fulfil it is excepted. But remaining a while breaches since wearing/riding continue by renewal. Entering has no continuation so staying inside is no breach (istiḥsān; qiyās: breach).tap to reveal
Does 'I will not eat head' refer to cows or sheep only?
وهذا اختلاف عصر وزمان كان العرف في زمنه فيهما وفي زمنهما في الغنم خاصة وفي زماننا يفتي على حسب العادة
AH: heads of cows and sheep; the Ṣāḥibayn: sheep only. The author says this is a difference of era: in AH's time custom covered both, in theirs sheep only; today the fatwā follows the custom.tap to reveal

Ḥadd al-Zinā I: proof and confession

Qudūrī lists the proofs of zinā. Hidāya supplies the definition of ḥadd, the Qurʾānic basis for four witnesses, the judge's interrogation (what, how, where, when, with whom) and the hadith of Māʿiz. Its big disputes: the Shāfiʿī accepts one confession where the Ḥanafīs require four in four sessions, and the Shāfiʿī (with Ibn Abī Laylā) holds that a retraction does not cancel the ḥadd. Hidāya's principle is idraʾū al-ḥudūd: any doubt averts the penalty, so even outward ʿadāla is not enough.
What is ḥadd and why are qiṣāṣ and taʿzīr not called ḥadd?
هو العقوبة المقدرة حقا لله تعالى حتى لا يسمى القصاص حدا لأنه حق العبد ولا التعزير لعدم التقدير
A fixed penalty as God's right; qiṣāṣ is a person's right, taʿzīr has no fixed measure. The core aim is deterrence from what harms people; purification is not the original aim, proved by its being prescribed on the kāfir too.tap to reveal
Why four witnesses?
ثُمَّ لَمْ يَأْتُوا بِأَرْبَعَةِ شُهَدَاءَ
Nisāʾ 15 and Nūr 4, and the Prophet's words to the man who accused his wife: bring four to testify to the truth of your claim. Rational point: four witnesses realise concealment (sitr), which is recommended, while spreading is its opposite.tap to reveal
What does the judge ask the witnesses?
يسألهم الإمام عن الزنا ما هو وكيف هو وأين زنى ومتى زنى وبمن زنى
What zinā is, how, where, when, with whom. The Prophet asked Māʿiz the manner and the woman. Caution is required: perhaps it was only contact outside the vulva, or in dār al-ḥarb, or long ago (taqādum), or there was a doubt unknown to all, like relations with the slave girl of his son.tap to reveal

Ḥadd al-Zinā II: how the ḥadd is carried out

Hidāya adds the manner of stoning and flogging with their evidence: who throws first, how long the whip, where not to strike, men standing and women seated, slaves half, the digging for women, who may execute (the master or the ruler), the conditions of iḥṣān (each justified by 'perfection of blessing'), and that rajm and jald are not combined, nor jald and exile (the Shāfiʿī joins them). It ends with postponement for the sick and pregnant.
What proves stoning?
لأنه ﵊ رجم ماعزا وقد أحصن وقال في الحديث المعروف وزنا بعد إحصان وعلى هذا إجماع الصحابة
The Prophet stoned Māʿiz, a muḥṣan; the well-known hadith 'zinā after iḥṣān'; and the consensus of the Companions. The Qurʾānic hundred is abrogated for the muḥṣan.tap to reveal
Do the witnesses begin the stoning?
وقال الشافعي ﵀ لا تشترط بداءته اعتبارا بالجلد. قلنا كل أحد لا يحسن الجلد
Ḥanafīs: witnesses begin, then the imam, then the people (as from ʿAlī), since a witness may be bold in testifying yet shrink from the act and retract. If witnesses refuse, the ḥadd lapses (sign of retraction). For a confessor the imam begins. Shāfiʿī: not required, by analogy with flogging. Reply: not everyone flogs well and a flogging might kill, which is not due, while stoning is destruction anyway.tap to reveal
Is the stoned person washed and prayed over?
اصنعوا به كما تصنعون بموتاكم
Yes, said of Māʿiz; also because he was killed rightfully, so washing does not lapse, as for one killed in retaliation; the Prophet prayed over al-Ghāmidiyya after she was stoned.tap to reveal

Ḥudūd: unions that do and do not require the ḥadd (shubha)

Hidāya's lasting contribution here is the doctrine of shubha: shubhat al-fiʿl (doubt of belief, needs a mistaken thought) and shubhat al-maḥall (ḥukmiyya, doubt from a real legal indication, no belief needed), with lists of eight and six cases. Beyond that Hidāya records AH vs the Ṣāḥibayn and al-Shāfiʿī on a contract on a mahram woman, sodomy, zinā in dār al-ḥarb, the musta'min, the zinā of a child or madman with a consenting woman, and compulsion.
What are the two kinds of shubha and how do they differ?
تتحقق بقيام الدليل النافي للحرمة في ذاته ولا تتوقف على ظن الجاني واعتقاده
Shubhat al-fiʿl (ishtibāh) exists only for a person who mistakes a non-proof for a proof, so belief is required. Shubhat al-maḥall (ḥukmiyya) exists when the proof itself negates the prohibition, whatever the doer thinks. Both suppress the ḥadd (hadith 'avert the ḥudūd by doubts'); lineage is established for the second, not the first, as in the first the act is pure zinā and the ḥadd drops only for a personal matter.tap to reveal
In which unions is the doubt in the act?
ففي هذه المواضع لا حد عليه إذا قال ظننت أنها تحل لي ولو قال علمت أنها علي حرام وجب الحد
Eight cases: slave girl of his father or mother or wife, a woman divorced thrice or irrevocably for payment while in ʿidda, an umm walad freed by her master during ʿidda, the master's slave girl for a slave, the mortgaged slave girl for the mortgagee (one report). No ḥadd if he says 'I thought it lawful'; if he says 'I knew it was forbidden', ḥadd.tap to reveal
In which unions is the doubt in the place (ḥukmiyya)?
ففي هذه المواضع لا يجب الحد وإن قال علمت أنها علي حرام
Six: the slave girl of his son, a woman irrevocably divorced by kināya, a sold slave girl for the seller before delivery, a slave girl given as dowry for the husband before receipt, a jointly-owned slave girl, the mortgaged slave girl (per Kitāb al-Rahn). No ḥadd even if he says he knew it was forbidden.tap to reveal

Ḥudūd: testimony on zinā and retraction

Qudūrī gives the rule that old testimony on ḥudūd is rejected. Hidāya adds its reasoning (the witness must choose between concealment and reporting; delay suggests grudge) and the difference between ḥudūd (not accepted after delay) and qadhf (accepted). It then catalogues the testimony disputes: contradictory witnesses on place or on force, blind or slave witnesses, who bears the penalty or blood-money (witnesses, treasury, validators) and how retraction works before and after execution.
Why does delay bar testimony in pure ḥudūd?
ولنا أن الشاهد مخير بين حسبتين أداء الشهادة والستر فالتأخير إن كان لاختيار الستر فالإقدام على الأداء بعد ذلك لضغينة هيجته
The witness chooses between two good deeds (testifying or concealing). If he delayed to conceal, coming forward now is probably from a grudge; if not, he is a sinner by silence; so a bar is certain. Shāfiʿī: not barred, by analogy to people's rights and to confession. Reply: confession is different as no one is hostile to himself.tap to reveal
If two witnesses say he forced her and two say she consented?
وله أنه اختلف المشهود عليه لأن الزنا فعل واحد يقوم بهما
AH and Zufar: no ḥadd on either. Ṣāḥibayn: ḥadd on the man only, as both agree on the cause. AH: the thing witnessed differs since zinā is one act by both; the witnesses of consent are accusers of her (qadhf), and are exempt only by the testimony of force, which removes her iḥṣān.tap to reveal
What if four witnesses are blind, qadhf-convicts, or a slave? And if they are fāsiqs?
لأن الفاسق من أهل الأداء والتحمل وإن كان في أدائه نوع قصور لتهمة الفسق
Blind/convicted/slave: the witnesses get the ḥadd for qadhf and the accused none, since they are not fit to testify. Fāsiqs: no ḥadd on either, because the fāsiq is a competent witness although defective (his judgment stands for us), which creates doubt of zinā, while defective fasq creates doubt of non-zinā. Fewer than four: qadhf.tap to reveal

Ḥadd al-Shurb (drinking)

Hidāya adds what Qudūrī leaves out: the hadith and the Companions' consensus behind the eighty lashes, the disputed length of taqādum (smell versus time), the difference between the khamr and the nabīdh, and the definition of the drunk. The main disputes: Muḥammad on confession after the smell has gone, Abū Yūsuf on two confessions, AH versus the Ṣāḥibayn on how deep drunkenness must be.
Is a drinker punished after the smell has gone?
لقول ابن مسعود ﵁ فيه فإن وجدتم رائحة الخمر فاجلدوه
AH and AY: no ḥadd after the smell goes (Ibn Masʿūd: 'if you find the smell of wine, flog him'), and for confession as well since the ḥadd rests on the Companions' consensus and there is no consensus except through Ibn Masʿūd's view. Muḥammad: ḥadd, since taqādum is fixed by time (as in zinā) and smell may come from elsewhere ('they say smell! you drank wine... I said no, I ate quince'); confession is not defeated by time.tap to reveal
Is the ḥadd for drunkenness from nabīdh?
لما روي أن عمر ﵁ أقام الحد على أعرابي سكر من النبيذ
Yes, ʿUmar punished a bedouin who became drunk on nabīdh. No ḥadd for someone merely smelling of wine or vomiting it, since the smell is ambiguous and drinking may be under compulsion.tap to reveal
What must be known before the drunk is punished?
لأن السكر من المباح لا يوجب الحد كالبنج ولبن الرماك
That he got drunk from nabīdh and drank willingly; drunkenness from a lawful substance (bhang, mare's milk) or compelled drinking brings no ḥadd. He is not punished until sober, for deterrence.tap to reveal

Ḥadd al-Qadhf and Taʿzīr

Hidāya adds the Qurʾānic proof of qadhf, conditions of iḥṣān of the slandered and the question of who may claim for a dead person. The central dispute is the nature of qadhf: for AH it is mainly God's right (so no inheritance, no pardon, no compensation, tadākhul), for al-Shāfiʿī mainly the person's right. It also gives the wording rulings (yā nabaṭī, ibn māʾ al-samāʾ, zanaʾta fi 'l-jabal), mutual accusations, liʿān, taʿzīr numbers (39 or 75) and the order of severity.
Is qadhf mainly God's right or the person's right?
ونحن صرنا إلى تغليب حق الشرع لأن ما للعبد من الحق يتولاه مولاه فيصير حق العبد مرعيا به
Both rights exist (it removes the victim's disgrace, yet it is a deterrent for cleaning the world of corruption). Ḥanafīs give priority to God's right; Shāfiʿī to the person's. Consequences for the Ḥanafīs: no inheritance of the claim, no valid pardon by the victim, no compensation, and tadākhul (many qadhfs merge); Shāfiʿī the opposite. Abū Yūsuf agrees with Shāfiʿī on pardon. Some Ḥanafīs say the person's right predominates; the first is clearer.tap to reveal
Who may claim the ḥadd for a dead person who was slandered?
ولا يطالب بحد القذف للميت إلا من يقع القدح في نسبه بقذفه وهو الوالد والولد
Only a parent or child, whose lineage is damaged. Shāfiʿī: every heir, since it is inherited. For the Ḥanafīs the claim is not by inheritance (so even a killer who is barred from inheritance has it, and a daughter's son, against Muḥammad; a grandchild while the son lives, against Zufar). The slandered man's death cancels the ḥadd (Shāfiʿī: not).tap to reveal
Which slandered persons are not muḥṣan?
والأصل فيه أن من وطىء حراما لعينه لا يجب الحد بقذفه لأن الزنا هو الوطء المحرم لعينه وإن كان محرما لغيره يحد

Kitāb al-Sariqa I: nisāb, proof and what is cut

Hidāya begins with the linguistic and juristic meaning of theft (secret taking) and adds the evidence and the disputes that Qudūrī passes over: the nisāb (ten dirhams versus Shāfiʿī's quarter dinar and Mālik's three dirhams), one confession versus two, and the list of exclusions. Hidāya's recurring logic is caution to avert the ḥadd (take the higher figure) and the principle that the thing must be property that no one can take openly or lawfully.
What is the minimum value for amputation?
ولنا أن الأخذ بالأكثر في هذا الباب أولى احتيالا لدرء الحد وهذا لأن في الأقل شبهة عدم الجناية
Ḥanafīs: ten dirhams (minted; weight seven mithqāls), by the hadith 'no cutting except for a dinar or ten dirhams'. Shāfiʿī: a quarter dinar; Mālik: three dirhams — both say the cut in the Prophet's time was only for the price of a shield, the least reported being three dirhams (a dinar was twelve dirhams). Reply: in this chapter caution requires the higher figure, since the lower contains a doubt of no real crime.tap to reveal
What is the Qurʾānic basis and why is a value threshold needed?
وَالسَّارِقُ وَالسَّارِقَةُ فَاقْطَعُوا أَيْدِيَهُمَا
Māʾida 38. Intellect and puberty are needed since there is no crime without them; a value threshold since desire is weak for trifles, taking them is not hidden, so neither the pillar nor the wisdom of deterrence is realised.tap to reveal
Is one confession enough for theft?
ولهما أن السرقة قد ظهرت بالإقرار مرة فيكتفي به كما في القصاص وحد القذف

Sariqa II: the ḥirz and the taking from it

The ḥirz is the other pillar. Hidāya explains the exemptions for relatives and spouses (relationships of free access), the difference between a place made for guarding (house, bath) and a place guarded by a person (mosque), the need to bring the goods out of the whole enclosure, and burglars working as a team. Disputes: Abū Yūsuf on relatives by fosterage and on putting the hand through a hole.
Why no cut for stealing from relatives?
فالأول وهو الولاد للبسوطة في المال وفي الدخول في الحرز
Parents and children: there is free use of each other's property and free entry into the guarded place; mahram kin: the same, shown by the Sharīʿa permitting looking at their adornment. Friends are different, since theft turned them into enemies. Shāfiʿī joins mahram kin to distant kin (see ʿitāq). Foster mahrams: AY no cut, because he enters without permission and with ease; ẓāhir al-riwāya: cut, since there is no kinship, and fosterage is little known so no free use.tap to reveal
Is the mosque a ḥirz?
لأنه محرز بالحافظ لأن المسجد ما بني لإحراز الأموال فلم يكن المال محرزا بالمكان
Yes, when the owner is beside his goods: it is guarded by a person, as a mosque was not built to guard property. Not so the bath and a house open to people, built for guarding, so the place itself is the ḥirz and a guard does not count. A guest does not get cut for stealing from his host, since the house is no longer a ḥirz in his regard.tap to reveal
Does he have to carry the goods out of the house?
لأن الدار كلها حرز واحد فلا بد من الإخراج منها
Yes, since the whole house is a single ḥirz. If it has apartments, bringing the goods from the apartment to the courtyard suffices because each apartment is a ḥirz for its occupant. A burglar's partner who takes them from his hand: istiḥsān cuts all, since the carrying is a joint action; qiyās (Zufar): only the carrier.tap to reveal

Sariqa III: how to cut, claims, and what changes in the stolen item

Hidāya explains the cut at the wrist with searing, the second theft (left foot) and why no third cut (ʿAlī's shame before God, agreed by the Companions), the effect of mistakes of the cutter, the owner's claim (even a depositary or usurper may claim; Zufar and Shāfiʿī deny) and what cancels the cut after judgment (gift, sale, drop in value). It then adds changes to the stolen object (tearing, dyeing).
Why is there no third cut?
ولنا قول علي ﵁ فيه إني لأستحي من الله تعالى أن لا أدع له يدا يأكل بها ويستنجي بها ورجلا يمشي عليها
ʿAlī: 'I am ashamed before God not to leave him a hand to eat and clean himself, and a foot to walk on'; he argued with the Companions and won, so it became consensus. It is also destruction in meaning (loss of the whole faculty), while ḥadd is a deterrent; and rare, while deterrence is for what is common. He is jailed until repentance. Shāfiʿī: cut again (third theft is as grave).tap to reveal
Can a depositary or usurper claim for the cut?
وقال زفر والشافعي رحمهما الله لا يقطع بخصومة الغاصب والمستودع
Ḥanafīs: yes, a depositary, usurper or one holding by ribā may demand it, and so may the owner. Zufar and Shāfiʿī: not by a usurper or depositary; same for borrower, tenant, muḍārib, mortgagee and anyone with a custodial hand other than the owner.tap to reveal
Does the thief's claim that the item is his drop the cut?
ولنا أن الشبهة دارئة تتحقق بمجرد الدعوى للاحتمال
Yes, even without proof: doubt averts the ḥadd and arises from the mere claim, since it is possible (retraction after confession is likewise accepted). Shāfiʿī: not by mere claim, as every thief could claim it and shut the door of the ḥadd.tap to reveal

Bāb Qaṭʿ al-Ṭarīq (highway robbery)

Hidāya explains the Qurʾānic punishments for highway robbery: imprisonment (the 'exile' of Māʾida 33) if they only frightened, hand-and-foot from opposite sides if they took ten dirhams per person, killing if they killed, and killing plus crucifixion for both. Kitāb al-Siyar I: jihād and the manner of fighting

Qudūrī gives the ruling of jihād and the conduct of war. Hidāya adds the definition of siyar, the Qurʾānic and ḥadīth basis of jihād as farḍ kifāya (and when the call becomes general it is farḍ ʿayn), the order of invitation (Islam, then jizya, then fighting) with the hadiths of the Prophet's commanders, and the rational ground for each rule: why one may shoot at a fortress even with Muslim prisoners inside, why no diya is owed, why women and Qurʾāns go only with a large safe army, and why one may not kill a parent who is an unbeliever except in self-defence.
What proves jihād and why is it farḍ kifāya?
الجهاد ماض إلى يوم القيامة
Tawba 36 and 'jihād continues till the Day of Resurrection'. It is not enjoined for itself (it is corruption in itself) but to raise the dīn of God and repel harm from people; so when some achieve it the rest are relieved, like funeral prayer and returning greetings. If nobody does it, all are sinful.tap to reveal
When does jihād become individually obligatory?
انْفِرُوا خِفَافًا وَثِقَالًا
When the call is general (nafīr ʿāmm): Tawba 41. Then the purpose is attained only by all, so it falls on all; the woman goes out without her husband's permission and the slave without his master's, since ownership and marriage do not appear against farḍ ʿayn, as in prayer and fasting.tap to reveal
Must the enemy be invited before fighting?
لما روى ابن عباس رضي الله عنما أن النبي ﵊ ما قاتل قوما حتى دعاهم إلى الإسلام
Yes, when the call has not reached them: Islam, then jizya (the Prophet's order to commanders; for apostates and Arab idolaters only Islam: 'you fight them or they submit'), then fighting. Fighting before the call is sinful but no compensation, since there is no protection (dīn or abode). For those the call reached it is recommended, not required (the Prophet raided Banū Muṣṭaliq unaware).tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Siyar III: spoils, captives, shares and tanfīl

This is the heart of Hidāya's siyar. It adds the imam's choices for a conquered land and captives (Khaybar versus the Sawād of Iraq), the principle that ownership of spoils is not complete until iḥrāz in dār al-Islām (a key Ḥanafī-Shāfiʿī dispute from which many cases follow), who takes a share (horse: two shares for AH, three for the Ṣāḥibayn's opponents, i.e. Shāfiʿī), the rāḍkh for non-fighters, the fifth and its recipients, and the tanfīl and salab (spoils of the slain).
What are the imam's options for a land conquered by force?
كذلك فعل عمر ﵁ بسواد العراق بموافقة من الصحابة ﵃
He may divide it among the Muslims (as the Prophet did at Khaybar) or leave its people with jizya on their persons and kharāj on their land (as ʿUmar did with the Sawād of Iraq, with the Companions' agreement). The former when the victors need, the latter otherwise, so that it serves future generations. Shāfiʿī: no pardon over land without a matching return; Reply: kharāj, though small now, is great in the end and they are like farmers who work for the Muslims.tap to reveal
May the imam show grace (mann) to captives?
ولأنه بالأسر والقسر ثبت حق الاسترقاق فيه فلا يجوز إسقاطه بغير منفعة وعوض

What to do with livestock that cannot be taken?
ولنا أن ذبح الحيوان يجوز لغرض صحيح ولا غرض أصح من كسر شوكة الأعداء

Weapons are burned and what cannot be burned buried.tap to reveal

Bāb Istīlāʾ al-Kuffār and Bāb al-Mustaʾmin

If non-Muslims seize Muslim property and take it to their land, Hidāya (against Shāfiʿī) says they own it, which leads to the owner's right to take it back before division free and after for the price. It also treats the runaway slave (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn), the Muslim merchant in enemy land (no treachery, but ruling on debts and usurpation there), and the musta'min in our land (one year, then jizya). Finally what is taken without fighting (fayʾ) is not subject to a fifth (against Shāfiʿī).
Do non-Muslims own Muslim property they seize and take home?
ولنا أن الاستيلاء ورد على مال مباح فينعقد سببا للملك دفعا لحاجة المكلف كاستيلائنا على أموالهم
Ḥanafīs: yes (istīlāʾ on permissible property is a cause of ownership, like ours over theirs); protection of property exists only while the owner can use it, and when he cannot, it reverts to permissibility. Bāb al-ʿUshr wa-l-Kharāj

Hidāya adds the proofs and reasoning behind the classification of land: Arabia is ʿushrī (no kharāj, since it is like fayʾ and the Arabs cannot be left in disbelief), Sawād is kharājī (ʿUmar), conquered land that is divided is ʿushrī, conquered land left to its people is kharājī. It then gives disputes on land revived from the dead (AY by its neighbourhood; Muḥammad by its water), on increasing the kharāj, and its reason for no ʿushr with kharāj on one land.
Why is Arabia ʿushrī?
لأنه بمنزلة الفيء فلا يثبت في أراضيهم كما لا يثبت في رقابهم
The Prophet and the Rightly Guided Caliphs took no kharāj from Arab lands; kharāj is like fayʾ and so is not laid on their lands as it is not laid on their persons; imposing it requires leaving the people in unbelief, and Arab polytheists get only Islam or the sword. ʿUmar placed it on the Sawād and Egypt and the Companions agreed on Syria.tap to reveal
Which land is ʿushrī and which kharājī?
لأن الحاجة إلى ابتداء التوظيف على المسلم والعشر أليق به لما فيه من معنى العبادة
Land whose people embraced Islam, or conquered and divided among the victors: ʿushr (the initial charge is on a Muslim, and ʿushr suits him as worship; also it is lighter, tied to the yield). Land conquered and left to its people, or by treaty: kharāj (initial charge on a kāfir). Mecca is exceptional: taken by force and left to its people without kharāj. al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr: conquered land reached by river water is kharājī; a spring dug is ʿushrī, since ʿushr attaches to growing land and its growth by its water.tap to reveal

ونهاية الطاقة أن يبلغ الواجب نصف الخارج لا يزاد عليه لأن التنصيف عين الإنصاف

Bāb al-Jizya and rulings on ahl al-dhimma

Hidāya adds the evidence and dispute on the amount of jizya (48/24/12 dirhams by wealth for the Ḥanafīs vs one dīnār for all for al-Shāfiʿī), who pays it (People of the Book, Magians, non-Arab idolaters), who is exempt, whether it lapses with conversion or death or merges over years, rules for new churches and dress, what breaks the covenant (cursing the Prophet: not for Ḥanafīs), Banū Taghlib's double ṣadaqa, and how the revenue is used.
What is the amount of jizya?
ومذهبنا منقول عن عمر وعثمان وعلي ﵃ ولم ينكر عليهم أحد من المهاجرين والأنصار ولأنه وجب نصرة للمقاتلة فتجب على التفاوت بمنزلة خراج الأرض
Ḥanafīs: 48 dirhams a year on the wealthy (4 monthly), 24 on the middle (2 monthly), 12 on the poor who work (1 monthly). Shāfiʿī: one dīnār (or equivalent) on each adult, rich or poor, by the Prophet's words to Muʿādh 'take from every adult a dīnār or its equivalent in maʿāfir', and because jizya replaces killing, which applies equally. Ḥanafīs: ʿUmar, ʿUthmān, ʿAlī set it graded without objection; it replaces personal and financial support, which varies; Muʿādh's report was a treaty (hence 'adult woman', who owes none).tap to reveal
Is jizya taken from non-Arab idolaters?
ولنا أنه يجوز استرقاقهم فيجوز ضرب الجزية عليهم إذ كل واحد منهما يشتمل على سلب النفس منهم
Yes: since they can be enslaved, jizya may be put on them (each takes away their self-rule: he works and pays Muslims, and his upkeep is from his earnings). Bāb Aḥkām al-Murtaddīn (apostasy)

Hidāya adds the evidence and sharp disputes on apostasy: whether the three-day respite is required (Shāfiʿī) or recommended, killing or imprisoning the female apostate (Shāfiʿī kills), status of the apostate's property (Abū Ḥanīfa: suspended; Ṣāḥibayn: owned), who inherits his earnings, his transactions (valid, void, suspended), what happens on joining dār al-ḥarb by judgment, and the apostasy and Islam of a discerning child (Zufar and Shāfiʿī reject both).
Must the apostate be given three days?
من غير قيد الإمهال وكذا قوله ﵊ من بدل دينه فاقتلوه
Invite him to Islam and remove any doubt (not obligatory, since the call has reached him). Imprisonment of three days is recommended (AH, AY), or must be given (Shāfiʿī: apostasy is apparently from doubt so a time to reflect is needed). Ḥanafīs: Tawba 5 without respite, 'whoever changes his religion, kill him', and he is a harbī kāfir reached by the call, so killed at once; one may not delay an obligation for a possible matter. Free or slave alike. His repentance is to disavow all religions but Islam, or what he moved to. If someone kills him before invitation it is disliked, no liability.tap to reveal
Is a female apostate killed?
ولنا أن النبي ﵊ نهى عن قتل النساء ولأن الأصل تأخير الجزية إلى دار الآخرة

Shāfiʿī kills her: apostasy is a grave crime meriting grave punishment, and hers equals his. Ḥanafīs: the Prophet forbade killing women; the original rule is deferring the punishment to the Hereafter; killing apostates is to repel an immediate harm (fighting) which a woman, lacking the build for it, does not pose.tap to reveal

Ṣāḥibayn: ownership continues, as he is a responsible, needy person, like one sentenced to stoning or qiṣāṣ.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Bughāt (rebels)

Hidāya adds the evidence and rationale for dealing with rebels (Muslims with a faulty ta'wīl and strength): invite them first (ʿAlī at Ḥarūrā), do not fight until they begin unless they gather (Khwāharzāda), finish off the wounded and pursue the fleeing only if they have a rallying force (Shāfiʿī: never), no enslaving of their families, using their weapons if needed (Shāfiʿī: no), and the status of what they take or kill (no liability; inheritance disputes).
May the imam start fighting the rebels?
ولنا أن الحكم يدار على الدليل وهو الاجتماع والامتناع وهذا لأنه لو انتظر الإمام حقيقة قتالهم ربما لا يمكنه الدفع
Qudūrī says he does not start; Hidāya says Khwāharzāda allows starting if they have camped and gathered. Shāfiʿī: not until they actually fight, since no Muslim may be killed except in defence, unlike a kāfir. Ḥanafīs: the ruling turns on the sign (gathering and refusing), as waiting might prevent repelling them. If news arrives that they buy arms, they are held until they repent. Before this the imam invites them and removes their doubt (ʿAlī did so at Ḥarūrā).tap to reveal
Are their families enslaved and wealth divided?
ولأنهم مسلمون والإسلام يعصم النفس والمال

Kitāb al-Laqīṭ (foundling)

Hidāya explains the foundling's freedom (the default of Adam's children, the abode of free people), maintenance from the treasury (ʿUmar and ʿAlī), and adds istiḥsān rules on claims of paternity: a claimant's word is accepted, descriptions of a bodily mark decide between two claimants, a dhimmī's claim makes him a dhimmī only for lineage, not for religion, and a slave's claim is accepted. Why is the foundling free and supported by the treasury?
لأن الأصل في بني آدم إنما هو الحرية وكذا الدار دار الأحرار ولأن الحكم للغالب
Freedom is the original state of Adam's children; the abode is of free people; the majority decides. His maintenance is from the treasury (ʿUmar and ʿAlī): a Muslim unable to earn with no property or kin, like a crippled man; his inheritance goes to the treasury (the gain goes with the liability) and his crime is paid from it. The finder is a volunteer unless the judge orders him so that it is a debt.tap to reveal
Is a claimant's word accepted that the foundling is his child?
وجه الاستحسان أنه إقرار للصبي بما ينفعه لأنه يتشرف بالنسب ويعير بعدمه

Is a reward due for returning a runaway slave?
ولنا أن الصحابة رضوان الله عليهم اتفقوا على وجوب أصل الجعل إلا أن منهم من أوجب أربعين ومنه من أوجب ما دونها

Ḥanafīs: the Companions agreed on the principle of reward, differing between forty and less, so forty for travel distance and less for less. Kitāb al-Mafqūd (missing person)

Hidāya explains the judge's custodianship for the missing man's money, maintenance for dependents (who is entitled without a judgment), the difference between a cause and its proof, and the dispute with Mālik (separate after four years) with the evidence of ʿAlī and ʿUmar's retraction. It adds the death age (120 per al-Ḥasan from AH, peers' death in the ẓāhir al-madhhab, 100 per AY, 90 more lenient), inheritance mechanics (the missing man and his heirs) and the example of two daughters and a missing son.
Why does the judge appoint a guardian for the missing man's property?
لأن القاضي نصب ناظرا لكل عاجز عن النظر لنفسه والمفقود بهذه الصفة وصار كالصبي والمجنون
The judge is appointed as overseer for everyone unable to look after himself, and the missing man is like a child or madman. The guardian collects his yields and debts admitted by his debtors, may sue for debts arising by his contract (he is principal), but not for debts the missing man handled through others, or his share in property in another's hand (he is only the judge's agent for receipt). Perishables are sold; non-perishables not, even for maintenance.tap to reveal
For whom is maintenance paid from the missing man's money?
والأصل أن كل من يستحق النفقة في ماله حال حضرته بغير قضاء القاضي ينفق عليه من ماله عند غيبته
Rule: whoever was entitled to maintenance in his presence without a judgment is maintained in his absence (wife, young children, adult daughters, disabled sons); those who need judgment only (brother, sister, maternal uncle or aunt) get none, since judgment against an absentee is barred. 'From his money' means cash (dirhams, dinars, bullion). If it is a deposit or debt, spend if the depositary and debtor admit the debt or deposit and the marriage and lineage, unless these are evident. If they deny, no claimant has standing.tap to reveal
Is the wife separated after a period?
ولنا قوله ﷺ في امرأة المفقود إنها امرأته حتى يأتيها البيان وقول علي ﵁ فيها هي امرأة ابتليت فلتصبر
No. Mālik: after four years the judge separates them, she observes the death-ʿidda and marries (ʿUmar's ruling in the case of the man abducted by jinn, by analogy to īlāʾ and ʿunna). Ḥanafīs: 'she is his wife until proof comes' and ʿAlī: 'a woman tested; let her be patient until death or divorce is clear'; marriage is established and absence does not dissolve it; death is a possibility, and certainty is not removed by doubt. ʿUmar retracted to ʿAlī's view. Īlāʾ differs (a deferred divorce), and ʿunna rarely ends after a year.tap to reveal
