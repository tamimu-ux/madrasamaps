Book of Criminal Offences (Homicide and Retaliation)

Al-Quduri opens the criminal law with a five-fold classification of killing (intentional, quasi-intentional, mistaken, treated-as-mistaken, and killing by cause) and ties each type to its consequence: sin, retaliation (qisas), blood-money (diya) and expiation (kaffara). He then sets out who may be retaliated against and who is excluded (e.g. a father for his son), the method of carrying out qisas, and the rules of retaliation for limbs and wounds, where equivalence (mumathala) is the controlling principle. The chapter ends with pardon/settlement and multiple offenders or victims. It leads straight into Kitab al-Diyat, which prices what qisas cannot reach.
How many kinds of killing does al-Quduri list and what are they?
القتل على خمسة أوجه: عمد وشبه عمد وخطإ وما أجزي مجرى الخطإ والقتل بسبب
Five: intentional, quasi-intentional, mistaken, treated-as-mistaken, and killing by cause.tap to reveal
What follows from intentional killing?
فالعمد: ما تعمد ضربه بسلاح أو ما أجزي مجرى السلاح في تفريق الأجزاء كالمحدد من الخشب والحجر والنار
Sin and qisas (unless the heirs pardon); no kaffara.tap to reveal
What is the position on shibh al-ʿamd and what does it require?
وشبه العمد عند أبي حنيفة: أن يتعمد الضرب بما ليس بسلاح ولا ما أجري مجرى السلاح وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد: إذا ضربه بحجر عظيم أو خشبة عظيمة فهو عمد وشبه العمد: أن يتعمد ضربه بما لا يقتل غالبا وموجب ذلك على القولين والمأثم والكفارة ولا قود وفيه دية مغلظة على العاقلة
Abu Hanifa: any non-weapon blow is shibh al-ʿamd. Abu Yusuf and Muhammad: a huge stone or club is ʿamd (qisas); shibh al-ʿamd is what does not usually kill. On both views: sin, kaffara, no qisas, aggravated diya on the ʿaqila.tap to reveal

Book of Blood-Money (Diyat)

Kitab al-Diyat fixes the price of a life and of each limb, the ten degrees of head wounds (shijaj), and who pays. Diya is 100 camels in the main schedule (aggravated in quarters for shibh al-ʿamd; in fifths for khata), with Abu Yusuf and Muhammad allowing cattle, goats and garments as alternative bases. Bodily organs follow a simple logic: a single organ, a pair, or a pair with four parts gets the full diya, and each member of a pair gets half. The chapter then covers wounds and compensation, fault in roads and animals, the slave's liability, collapsing walls, the fetus (ghurra) and kaffara.
What is the diya for shibh al-ʿamd and who pays it?
إذا قتل رجل رجلا عمد فعلى عاقلته دية مغلظة وعليه كفارة ودية شبه العمد عند أبي حنيفة وأبي يوسف مائة من الإبل أرباعا: خمس وعشرون بنت مخاض وخمس وعشرون بنت لبون وخمس وعشرون حقة وخمس وعشرون جذعة
100 camels in quarters (25 each of bint makhad, bint labun, hiqqa, jadha'a), on the aqila, plus kaffara on the killer. (The printed text reads ʿamd for shibh al-ʿamd in its first clause.)tap to reveal
Is aggravation possible in other than camels?
التغليظ إلا في الإبل خاصة فإن قضي بالدية من غير الإبل لم تتغلظ
No.tap to reveal
What is the diya for a mistaken killing and what bases are allowed?
والدية في الخطأ مائة من الإبل أخماسا: عشرون بنت مخاض وعشرون ابن مخاض وعشرون بنت لبون وعشرون حقة
100 camels in fifths of 20 (the fifth kind, jadha'a, is omitted in the printed text). Abu Hanifa allows only camels, dinars (1000) or dirhams (10,000); Abu Yusuf and Muhammad add 200 cows, 2000 goats, 200 garments. Muslim and dhimmi diya are equal.tap to reveal

Chapter of Qasama (Oaths for an Unsolved Killing)

When a body is found in a locality and no one knows the killer, qasama assigns the burden to the locality itself: fifty of its men, chosen by the victim's guardian, swear that they neither killed him nor know the killer, and the inhabitants then pay the diya. The Hanafi school stresses the place and who has control of it, so the chapter goes through special places: a house, a boat, a mosque, a main road, wasteland and a river. It closes with what happens if the guardian accuses someone specific.
What is the procedure and consequence of qasama?
وإذا وجد القتيل في محلة ولا يعلم من قتله استحلف خمسون رجلا منهم
Fifty men of the locality, chosen by the guardian, swear: "By God, we did not kill him nor know his killer." Then diya is imposed on the locality. The guardian does not swear and the locality is not judged to owe qisas. If there are fewer than fifty the oaths repeat.tap to reveal
Who is excluded from the qasama?
ولا يدخل في القسامة صبي ولا مجنون
Children, the insane, women and slaves.tap to reveal
What if the body has no mark?
وإن وجد ميت لا أثر به فلا قسامة ولا دية
No qasama and no diya, unless blood flows from eyes or ears.tap to reveal

Chapter of the ʿAqila (Those Who Bear the Diya)

The chapter defines the ʿaqila: first the people of the state register (diwan), whose stipends fund the diya over three years, then the kinsfolk of the offender's tribe. It sets floors and caps and says what the aqila never bear: a slave's offence, an admitted crime, or a settlement.
Who are the aqila?
والعاقلة: أهل الديوان
The ahl al-diwan; for one outside the diwan, his tribe.tap to reveal
How is the diya paid?
إن كان القاتل من أهل الديوان يؤخذ من عطاياهم في ثلاث سنين
From stipends in three years; for tribesmen, divided over three years, no one paying more than four dirhams per year; the nearest tribe is added if the tribe cannot manage; the killer joins like one of them.tap to reveal
Is there a floor?
ولا تتحمل العاقلة أقل من نصف عشر الدية وتتحمل نصف العشر فصاعدا وما ينقص من ذلك فهو في مال الجاني
They bear a twentieth or more; less comes from the offender.tap to reveal

Book of Hudud: Zina

The Book of Hudud begins with zina, the hadd crime with the strictest proof: four witnesses, or four confessions in four separate sittings, followed by a probing interrogation by the judge. The punishment depends on ihsan: stoning for the muhsan, a hundred lashes for the free unmarried, fifty for a slave. The chapter then covers retraction, who may carry out the hadd, the conditions of ihsan, the pregnant and the sick, delayed testimony, and the many situations of doubt (shubha) that avert the hadd, which is the principle governing the whole book.
How is zina proven?
الزنا يثبت بالبينة والإقرار
Only by testimony or confession.tap to reveal
What is required of the witnesses?
فالبينة: أن تشهد أربعة من الشهود على رجل أو امرأة بالزنا فيسألهم الإمام عن الزنا
Four witnesses; the judge asks what zina is, how, where, with whom, when; they must say they saw it (like the kohl-stick in the kohl-pot) and be vetted in private and public.tap to reveal
How must confession be made?
والإقرار: أن يقر البالغ العاقل على نفسه بالزنا أربع مرات في أربعة مجالس من مجالس المقر كلما أقر رده القاضي
A sane adult confesses four times in four separate sittings; the judge dismisses him each time, then questions him fully.tap to reveal

Chapter of the Hadd for Drinking

The drinking hadd is eighty lashes for the free (forty for the slave), but is limited by strict conditions: the smell must be present, the intoxication must be from nabidh drunk willingly, and he must be sober at the time of lashing. Proof is by two male witnesses or a single confession.
When is the hadd for khamr due?
ومن شرب الخمر فأخذ وريحها موجود فشهد الشهود بذلك عليه أو أقل فعليه الحد وإن أقر بعد ذهاب رائحتها لم يحد
If caught with the smell present and witnessed; no hadd if he confesses after the smell has gone.tap to reveal
What about nabidh?
ومن سكر من النبيذ حد
Hadd if intoxicated.tap to reveal
Is smell alone enough?
ولا حد على من وجد منه رائحة الخمر أو تقيأها ولا يحد السكران حتى يعلم أنه سكر من النبيذ وشربه طوعا ولا يحد حتى يزول عنه السكر
No; hadd only for willing drinking known to be intoxicating; waits for sobriety.tap to reveal

Chapter of the Hadd for Slander (Qadhf) and Taʿzir

Qadhf is false accusation of zina against a chaste person. The hadd is eighty lashes (forty for a slave), imposed on the claim of the slandered person. The chapter defines ihsan for qadhf (free, sane, adult, Muslim, chaste), the exact words that count as qadhf (denying lineage of a person whose mother is dead and chaste), and the words that only carry taʿzir. It then gives the limits of taʿzir and the hierarchy of severity among punishments, and ends with the loss of testimony.
What is the hadd for qadhf?
إذا قذف رجل رجلا محصنا أو امرأة محصنة بصريح الزنا وطالب المقذوف بالحد حده الحاكم ثمانين سوطا إن كان حرا يفرق على أعضائه ولا يجرد عن ثيابه غير أنه ينزع عنه الفرو والحشو وإن كان عبدا جلده أربعين
Eighty lashes for a free man (forty for a slave), distributed, with only fur and padded clothing removed, if the victim demands.tap to reveal
What is ihsan in qadhf?
والإحصان: أن يكون المقذوف حرا عاقلا بالغا مسلما عفيفا عن فعل الزنا
Free, sane, adult, Muslim, chaste.tap to reveal
Is denying someone's lineage qadhf?
ومن نفى نسب غيره فقال: لست لأبيك أو: يا ابن الزانية وأمه ميتة محصنة وطالب الابن بالحد حد القاذف
Yes: he is lashed if the son claims, because it slanders the chaste mother; only someone whose lineage is touched may claim for a dead person.tap to reveal

Book of Theft (Sariqa) and Highway Robbery

The hadd of theft (amputation of the right hand) is hedged by many conditions. The thief must be a sane adult who takes secretly at least ten dirhams from a proper hirz (protected place) belonging to someone else, and the owner must claim. Everything else is a doubt: items of trivial or perishable value, things with an owner's share, theft between relatives, items taken from an unprotected place, and snatching or embezzlement. The Book then gives the manner of amputation, the second and third offences, and the law of highway robbers (qutta al-tariq), whose punishment depends on whether they killed, took money, or both.
What are the conditions of the amputation penalty?
إذا سرق العاقل البالغ عشرة دارهم أو ما قيمته عشرة دراهم مضروبة أو غير مضروبة من حرز لا شبهة فيه وجب عليه القطع
Sane adult steals ten dirhams or its value from a hirz with no doubt.tap to reveal
How is the theft proven, and is status relevant?
ويجب القطع بإقراره مرة واحدة أو بشهادة شاهدين
One confession or two witnesses; slave and free are the same.tap to reveal
If a group steals together?
وإذا اشترك جماعة في سرقة فأصاب كل واحد منهم عشرة دراهم قطع وإن أصابه أقل من ذلك لم يقطع
Each is cut only if his share reaches ten dirhams.tap to reveal

Book of Beverages

The Hanafi division of drinks turns on four prohibited kinds: khamr (raw grape juice that has fermented and foamed), grape juice cooked down to less than two thirds, date or raisin infusion that has fermented, and cooked date or raisin juice that has fermented hard. Drinks outside these four are lawful in a quantity that does not intoxicate and when drunk without frivolity. The chapter ends with the permission to turn wine into vinegar.
Which drinks are forbidden?
الأشربة المحرمة أربعة: الخمر
Four: khamr; grape juice cooked down but by less than two thirds; the raw infusion (naqiʿ) of dates and raisins once strong; and nabidh of dates and raisins cooked only lightly. The text then says the nabidh types are lawful, even if strong, when one drinks an amount he does not expect to intoxicate him, without frivolity or revelry.tap to reveal
Which drinks are lawful?
ولا بأس بالخليطين ونبيذ العسل والتين والحنطة والشعير والذرة حلال وإن لم يطبخ
Mixtures of two nabidh, and nabidh of honey, fig, wheat, barley and millet are lawful even uncooked (so long as not drunk for intoxication).tap to reveal
What of juice cooked down to a third?
وعصير العنب إذا طبخ حتى ذهب منه ثلثاه وبقي ثلثه حلال وإن اشتد ولا بأس بالانتباذ في الدباء والحنتم والمزفت والنقير
Lawful even if it strengthens; and no harm in making nabidh in gourd, pitch-coated and hollowed vessels.tap to reveal

Book of Hunting (Sayd)

Hunting is lawful with trained animals (dog, cheetah, falcon) or with arrows, provided the hunter is eligible to slaughter, mentions God's name at release, and the animal kills by wounding. The chapter defines training for dogs and hawks, sets out when the catch is eaten or not (the dog eats; a second untrained or Magian dog joins; the animal strangles without wounding), then goes through arrows (falls in water, falls from a roof, ricochets), the blunt mi'rad and bunduq, and severed limbs. It ends with competing hunters.
With what may one hunt?
يجوز الاصطياد بالكلب المعلم والفهد والبازي وسائر الجوارح المعلمة
With a trained dog, cheetah, falcon, and any trained predatory animal.tap to reveal
How is training defined?
وتعليم الكلب: أن يترك الأكل ثلاث مرات
Dog: leaves eating three times; falcon: returns when called.tap to reveal
When is the catch of a trained animal eaten?
فإذا أرسل كلبه العلم أو بازيه أوصقره وذكر اسم الله تعالى عليه عند إرساله فأخذ الصيد وجرحه فمات حل أكله
If sent with God's name and it wounds and the game dies: lawful. If the dog eats, not; if a falcon eats, lawful; if found alive he must slaughter it; strangled without wounding, not eaten.tap to reveal

Book of Slaughter (Dhaba'ih)

Having covered hunting, al-Quduri treats slaughter proper: who may slaughter (Muslim or Kitabi, not a Magian, apostate or idolater), the basmala, the throat cut and which veins count (Abu Hanifa: three of four; the two: three including both windpipe and gullet), permitted tools, the sunna of nahr and dhabh, and the fetus. Book of the Udhiya (Sacrificial Animal)

The Hanafis hold udhiya to be wajib on every free, resident, wealthy Muslim on the Day of Sacrifice, for himself and his minor children. The chapter gives the times (from dawn of Nahr, after the Eid prayer in cities, three days), the animals and ages, the defects that disqualify, the sharing of cattle and camels among seven, and the distribution of the meat.
Who must make udhiya?
الأضحية واجبة على كل حر مسلم مقيم موسر في يوم الأضحى عن نفسه وولده الصغار
Every free, Muslim, resident, wealthy person for himself and his minor children; not the poor or the traveller.tap to reveal
What animal and how many shares?
أو يذبح بدنة أو بقرة عن سبعة وليس على الفقير والمسافر أضحية
A sheep per person, or a camel or cow for seven; valid on three days: the Day of Nahr and the two after .tap to reveal

ووقت الأضحية يدخل بطلوع الفجر من يوم النحر إلا أنه لا يجوز لأهل الأمصار الذبح حتى يصلي الإمام صلاة العيد فأما أهل السواد فيذبحون بعد الفجر

Book of Oaths (Aymaan): Kinds, Wording, Kaffara

Oaths are of three kinds: ghamus (a deliberate lie about the past; sin, no kaffara, only istighfar), mun'aqida (about a future act; kaffara on breach) and laghw (about a past matter wrongly believed true; hoped to be forgiven). Only an oath by God, one of His names or an attribute of His essence counts; swearing by the Prophet, the Qur'an or the Kaʿba does not. Particles of oath, formulas that count and those that do not, the kaffara (free a slave, clothe or feed ten poor people, else fast three days), oaths on sin, tahrim, and vows are all treated.
What are the three kinds of oath?
الأيمان على ثلاثة أضرب: يمين غموس ويمين منعقدة ويمين لغو
Ghamus, mun'aqida and laghw.tap to reveal
What of the ghamus?
فهذه اليمين يأثم بها صاحبها ولا كفارة فيها إلا الاستغفار
Sin; no kaffara other than istighfar.tap to reveal
What of the mun'aqida?
واليمين المنعقدة: هي الحلف على الأمر المستقبل أن يفعله أو لا يفعله فإذا حنث في ذلك لزمته الكفارة
Kaffara upon breach.tap to reveal

Applications of Oaths: When Is the Oath Broken?

The second half of Kitab al-Aymaan is a catalogue of cases deciding whether an oath was broken. The method is to look first at the word's literal meaning and the speaker's intent, then at custom (ʿurf): swearing not to enter a house, wear a garment, eat meat, speak, buy and sell, marry, or sit on a bed each produce rulings. The key distinctions are between delegating a deed and doing it oneself, between the object changing form or ownership, and between the day/time words (hin, ayyam) whose lengths are fixed by convention.
Swearing not to enter a house?
ومن خلف لا يدخل بيتا فدخل الكعبة أو المسجد أو البيعة أو الكنيسة لم يحنث
Entering the Ka'ba, a mosque, a church or synagogue is no breach: they are not 'houses' by custom for living.tap to reveal
Reciting in prayer after swearing not to speak?
ومن حلف لا يتكلم فقرأ في الصلاة لم يحنث
No breach.tap to reveal
Already wearing, or riding?
ومن حلف لا يلبس ثوبا وهو لابسه فنزعه في الحال
No breach if he removes it at once; the same for dismounting immediately; if he stays a while, breach; swearing not to enter a house while inside: no breach by sitting until he leaves and re-enters.tap to reveal

1Can the confessor retract?
Yes, before or during the hadd; he is released. It is recommended to prompt him: "perhaps you only touched or kissed".
2If a wound keeps the victim bedridden until death, is it qisas?
Yes: the wound is deemed the cause of death.
3What is the oath wording when the accused names someone?
"By God, I did not kill him, nor know any killer other than so-and-so."
4Which organs carry the full diya?
Life, nose-tip, tongue, penis, intellect, a beard that does not regrow; likewise (continuing) hair of the head, eyebrows, two eyes, hands, feet, ears, lips, testicles, women's brea
5What makes a valid oath?
By God, a name of His or an attribute of His essence (his glory, majesty); not 'ilm Allah, nor attributes of action like His anger.
6What if the guardian accuses a particular man?
Accusing a resident leaves the qasama on them; accusing an outsider drops it.
7What items carry no amputation?
Trivial, normally permissible items (wood, reeds, grass, fish, birds, game), and perishables like fresh fruit, milk, meat, melons, unharvested crops.
8If a group steals together?
Each is cut only if his share reaches ten dirhams.
9Foods of one species?
Meat does not include fish; unripe-date oath: ripe dates do not breach; wheat versus its bread: no breach; flour versus its bread: breach; flour eaten raw: no.
10May a slanderer retract his confession?
No: qadhf includes a right of the human being.
