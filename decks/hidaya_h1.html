Muqaddima — the author's preface

Marghīnānī explains that he first began a long commentary (Kifāyat al-Muntahā) on his own Bidāyat al-Mubtadī, then abandoned it as too prolix and wrote al-Hidāya instead, joining 'the sources of transmission with the principles of understanding' (riwāya and dirāya). The method is therefore: each Bidāya ruling is followed by its dalīl and the school's disputes, without padding. This is why Hidāya is the textbook of khilāf and istidlāl in Dars-e-Nizami.
What method does the author announce for al-Hidāya?
أجمع فيه بتوفيق الله تعالى بين عيون الرواية ومتون الدراية تاركا للزوائد في كل باب
He joins narrated sources with rational principles and omits surplus detail in every chapter.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Wuḍūʾ (incl. nawāqiḍ, ghusl)

Hidāya grounds the farāʾiḍ of wuḍūʾ in Qurʾān 5:6 and then spends its energy on the disputes with al-Shāfiʿī (niyya, tartīb, extent of masḥ, wiping the ears, ghusl rinsing) and Zufar (whether the elbows are included). The faṣl on nawāqiḍ builds the central argument: what exits the body invalidates wuḍūʾ by qiyās on the sabīlayn plus the ḥadīth 'al-wuḍūʾ min kulli damin sāʾil', with Ḥanafī measures (sayalān, mil' al-fam). The faṣl on ghusl lists farāʾiḍ, sunan and mūjibāt, with al-Shāfiʿī's disagreement on the manī and rinsing of mouth and nose.

Bāb al-Māʾ (waters; well; leftovers)

This bāb defines which water purifies. The Ḥanafī position is that mutlaq water and water mixed with a pure thing that does not take away its name still purifies; water into which any najāsa falls is impure (against Mālik and the two-qulla ḥadīth of al-Shāfiʿī) unless it is flowing or a large pool (ten by ten cubits). The 'used water' (māʾ mustaʿmal) dispute has four shapes (AH/AY, Muḥammad, Zufar, Mālik/Shāfiʿī). The faṣl on wells is built on āthār, not qiyās; the faṣl on leftovers (asʾār) ranks animals by the purity of their saliva, with the famous nabīdh al-tamr issue closing it.
What waters purify?
الطهارة من الأحداث جائزة بماء السماء والأودية والعيون والآبار والبحا ر
Sky, valley, spring, well and sea water.tap to reveal
Is water mixed with saffron, milk or soap still purifying?
وتجوز الطهارة بماء خالطه شيء طاهر فغير أحد أوصافه
Yes, as long as its name 'water' remains and the mixture does not dominate by parts.tap to reveal
Does a little impurity spoil standing water?
وكل ماء وقعت فيها لنجاسة لم يجز الوضوء به قليلا كانت النجاسة أو كثيرا
Yes, any amount, unless the water is flowing or a large pool.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Tayammum

Tayammum is the substitute when water is not found (a mile away), or its use would harm (illness, cold), based on Qurʾān 4:43. Hidāya's contribution is the disputes: what substances are 'ṣaʿīd' (AH/Muḥammad: any earth-genus; AY: soil and sand; Shāfiʿī: fertile soil), whether niyya is farḍ (Zufar says no), whether tayammum is valid for one prayer or many (Shāfiʿī: each farḍ), and the cases of one who forgot water in his saddlebag or tayammums before searching.
When may a person do tayammum?
ومن لم يجد ماء وهو مسافر أو خارج المصر بينه وبين المصر نحو ميل أو أكثر يتيمم بالصعيد
No water, traveller or about a mile outside the city; or sick fearing worsening.tap to reveal
Junub fears cold kills him: tayammum inside a city?
ولو كان في المصر فكذلك عند أبي حنيفة رحمه الله تعالى خلافا لهما
AH: yes, as inability is real. The two: no, rare in cities.tap to reveal
Method of tayammum?
التيمم ضربتان: ضربة للوجه، وضربة لليدين
Two strikes, face and arms to the elbows, covering everything.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Masḥ ʿalā al-Khuffayn

Masḥ on the khuffs is a rukhṣa to remove hardship from repeated removal. Hidāya sets the area (three fingers of the hand), tears allowed (less than three toes), who may wipe (not after janāba), what breaks it, travel-and-residence switching, and the two extensions: jarmūq (yes) and jawrabayn (AH: only if leathered; the two and later AH: if thick). Jabāʾir are wiped with no time limit.
How much of the khuff is wiped?
وفرض ذلك مقدار ثلاث أصابع من أصابع اليد
Three fingers of the hand.tap to reveal
A tear in the khuff?
ولا يجوز المسح على خف فيه خرق كبير يبين منه قدر ثلاث أصابع من أصابع الرجل فإن كان أقل من ذلك جاز
Less than three toes: fine. Three or more (smallest toes): not, counted per khuff.tap to reveal
May a junub wipe the khuffs?
أن لا ننزع خفافنا ثلاثة أيام ولياليها لا عن جنابة
No: only for ḥadath, not janāba.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Ḥayḍ wa-l-Istiḥāḍa (incl. nifās)

Hidāya anchors the Ḥanafī limits of ḥayḍ (3 to 10 days) and nifās (up to 40 days) in ḥadīth, and replies to al-Shāfiʿī's 1 day to 15 days and 60 days. It lists the prohibitions of the ḥāʾiḍ (prayer, fasting, mosque, ṭawāf, intercourse, Qurʾān recitation, touching the muṣḥaf), rules for ending of blood before and after ten days, istiḥāḍa as a continuing ʿudhr with wuḍūʾ per prayer-time (Shāfiʿī: per farḍ), and a khilāf on twins in nifās.
What are the minimum and maximum of ḥayḍ?
أقل الحيض ثلاثة ايام ولياليها وما نقص من ذلك فهو استحاضة
Min three days with nights; max ten; extra or less is istiḥāḍa.tap to reveal
Are yellow and turbid discharge ḥayḍ?
وما تراه المرأة من الحمرة والصفرة والكدرة في أيام الحيض حيض
Yes, until pure white; green for habitual menstruators.tap to reveal
Prayer and fasting while menstruating?
والحيض يسقط عن الحائض الصلاة ويحرم عليها الصوم وتقضي الصوم ولا تقضي الصلاة
Prayer lapses, fasting forbidden and made up.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Anjās (incl. istinjāʾ)

Hidāya states that removing najāsa from body, garment and place is wājib. The big disputes: what may remove it (any removing liquid per AH/AY; only water per Muḥammad, Zufar, Shāfiʿī); how dried najāsa on the khuff is cleansed (rubbing, istiḥsān); mani (rubbing vs Shāfiʿī's purity); dirham measure for 'mughallaẓa' and quarter for 'mukhaffafa', with difference between AH and the Ṣāḥibayn on dung and horse urine. The faṣl on istinjāʾ: stones suffice without count against Shāfiʿī's three.
Is removing najāsa required?
تطهير النجاسة واجب من بدن المصلي وثوبه والمكان الذي يصلي عليه
Yes, body, clothes and place.tap to reveal
Can najāsa be removed by vinegar or rosewater?
ويجوز تطهيرها بالماء وبكل مائع طاهر يمكن إزالتها به كالخل وماء الورد ونحوه مما إذا عصر إنعصر
AH and AY: yes, any pure liquid that removes it.tap to reveal
Dry najāsa with bulk on the khuff?
وإذا أصاب الخف نجاسة لها جرم كالروث والعذرة والدم والمني فجفت فدلكه بالأرض جاز
Rubbing on ground suffices (istiḥsān, AH/AY).tap to reveal

Bāb al-Mawāqīt (times of prayer; disliked times)

The Kitāb al-Ṣalāh opens with times, proved by the imāmat of Jibrīl over two days. The main disputes: the end of Ẓuhr (AH: two shadows; Ṣāḥibayn and Shāfiʿī: one), the shafaq (AH: whiteness; Ṣāḥibayn and Shāfiʿī: redness), the end of Maghrib (Shāfiʿī: only three rakʿas' time), and the end of ʿIshāʾ. Faṣl on recommended times (isfār of Fajr, ibrād of Ẓuhr, delaying ʿAṣr, hastening Maghrib, delaying ʿIshāʾ) argues against al-Shāfiʿī's general ta'jīl; the faṣl on makrūh times covers sunrise, zenith and sunset and which prayers are excepted.
When does Fajr begin and end?
أول وقت الفجر إذا طلع الفجر الثاني وهو البياض المعترض في الأفق وآخر وقتها ما لم تطلع الشمس
From the true (spreading) dawn until sunrise; the false dawn (vertical white then dark) does not count.tap to reveal
When does Ẓuhr end?
وآخر وقتها عند أبي حنيفة ﵀ إذا صار ظل كل شيء مثليه سوى فيء الزوال وقالا إذا صار الظل مثله
AH: when the shadow is twice the object (excluding zawāl shade). Ṣāḥibayn: once.tap to reveal
How long does Maghrib last?
وأول وقت المغرب إذا غربت الشمس وآخر وقتها مالم يغب الشفق
From sunset until the twilight disappears.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Adhān

Adhān is a sunna for the five prayers and Jumuʿa, narrated mutawātiran. Hidāya's disagreements with al-Shāfiʿī: tarjīʿ (no), iqāma (twice, not once), qaḍāʾ prayers (adhān and iqāma). Within Ḥanafīs: sitting between adhān and iqāma in Maghrib (AH: no; Ṣāḥibayn: a light sitting), taṭwīb (Kufan innovation, accepted in Fajr), and performing adhān before time (AY and Shāfiʿī: permitted in the last half of night for Fajr).

Like adhān plus 'qad qāmat al-ṣalāh' twice.tap to reveal

Bāb Shurūṭ al-Ṣalāh (conditions before prayer)

Conditions before prayer: purity from ḥadath and najāsa, covering the ʿawra, intention, and qibla. Key: the navel is not ʿawra and the knee is (against Shāfiʿī); the quarter measure for ʿawra and for najāsa; the praying of one without cloth or with an impure cloth; the niyya must not be separated from takbīr; the person lost in the dark with qibla.
What is the man's ʿawra?
وعورة الرجل ما تحت السرة إلى الركبة
From below the navel to the knee; knee included.tap to reveal
Free woman's ʿawra?
وبدن الحرة كلها عورة إلا وجهها وكفيها
All except face and hands (the feet: sounder view that they are not ʿawra).tap to reveal
How much exposure invalidates prayer?
فإن صلت وربع ساقها أو ثلثه مكشوف تعيد الصلاة
A quarter of a limb or more: repeat. Less: no.tap to reveal
