Kitāb al-Nikāḥ — the contract and witnesses

Hidāya's opening of Nikāḥ adds almost no new rulings to Qudūrī; its contribution is the reasoning. It explains why past-tense wording creates a contract (inshāʾ), why the word 'tamlīk' or 'hiba' can conclude nikāḥ by majāz (al-Shāfiʿī restricts to nikāḥ/tazwīj), and why witnesses need not be just (against al-Shāfiʿī) nor male-only. It also adds a far' on a father present while an agent marries off his minor daughter.
Which words conclude nikāḥ — only nikāḥ/tazwīj, or also hiba, tamlīk, ṣadaqa?
وقال الشافعي ﵀ لا ينعقد إلا بلفظ النكاح والتزويج لأن التمليك ليس حقيقة فيه ولا مجازا عنه
Ḥanafīs: hiba, tamlīk and ṣadaqa conclude nikāḥ; al-Shāfiʿī: only nikāḥ and tazwīj.tap to reveal
Does the word bayʿ (sale) conclude nikāḥ, and ijāra, ibāḥa, waṣiyya?
وينعقد بلفظ البيع هو الصحيح لوجود طريق المجاز ولا ينعقد بلفظ الإجارة في الصحيح لأنه ليس بسبب لملك المتعة
Bayʿ concludes it (ṣaḥīḥ); ijāra, ibāḥa, iḥlāl, iʿāra and waṣiyya do not.tap to reveal
What is the evidence that witnesses are a condition of nikāḥ?
لقوله ﵊ لا نكاح إلا بشهود وهو حجة على مالك ﵀ في اشتراط الإعلان دون الشهادة
Hadith "no nikāḥ without witnesses" is the proof, and it refutes Mālik who requires only public announcement (iʿlān).tap to reveal

Faṣl: Prohibited women (muḥarramāt)

Qudūrī lists the prohibited women; Hidāya supplies each Qurʾānic and hadith source and the main Shāfiʿī disputes: whether zinā or lust-touch creates muṣāhara, whether marrying the sister of a woman in her ʿidda is allowed, marriage in iḥrām, the Kitābiyya slave woman, the limit of slaves and free men, temporary marriage, and the pregnant woman from zinā. Ḥanafī reasoning centres on 'sabab' (a cause leading to the child) and ihtiyāṭ.
Does zinā with a woman make her mother and daughter ḥarām for the man?
ولنا أن الوطء سبب الجزئية بواسطة الولد حتى يضاف إلى كل واحد منهما كملا
Ḥanafīs: yes; Shāfiʿī: zinā does not create muṣāhara.tap to reveal
Do touching or looking with lust create muṣāhara?
ولنا أن المس والنظر سبب داع إلى الوطء فيقام مقامه في موضع الاحتياط
Ḥanafīs: touch with desire (and looking at the inner vulva) makes her mother and daughter forbidden; Shāfiʿī: no.tap to reveal
Why is the aunt (ʿamma/khāla) not combined with her niece, and how is a ḥadīth allowed to add to the Qurʾān?
لا تنكح المرأة على عمتها ولا على خالتها ولا على ابنة أخيها أو ابنة أختها وهذا مشهور تجوز الزيادة على الكتاب بمثله
Ḥadīth of the aunt/niece; since it is mashhūr it can add to the Book.tap to reveal

Bāb: Guardians (awliyāʾ) and guardianship

Hidāya's heart in this bāb is the dispute over whether a mature free woman can contract her own marriage without a walī (Ḥanafī/ Abū Yūsuf/ Muḥammad/ Mālik/ Shāfiʿī), over compulsion (ijbār) of the adult virgin, and whether silence is consent. It adds the Ḥanafī view that kinship is the cause of wilāya by 'concern' (shafaqa), the khiyār al-bulūgh dispute (AY vs AH/Muḥammad), the order of guardians, the wilāya of non-ʿaṣaba relatives (AH vs Muḥammad), and absence of the nearest guardian (Zufar).
May a mature free woman contract her own marriage without a walī?
ووجه الجواز أنها تصرفت في خالص حقها وهي من أهله لكونها عاقلة مميزة ولهذا كان لها التصرف في المال ولها اختيار الأزواج
AH and AY (ẓāhir al-riwāya): valid. AY (another report): only with walī. Muḥammad: valid but suspended on the walī. Mālik and al-Shāfiʿī: void.tap to reveal
May a walī force an adult virgin to marry?
ولنا أنها حرة مخاطبة فلا يكون للغير عليها ولاية الإجبار
Ḥanafīs: no. Al-Shāfiʿī: yes, by analogy with the minor, because she is inexperienced and the father may take her mahr without her order.tap to reveal
What is the evidence that a virgin's silence is consent?
البكر تستأمر في نفسها فإن سكتت فقد رضيت ولأن جنية الرضا فيه راجحة لأنها تستحيي عن إظهار الرغبة لا عن الرد
Hadith: the virgin is consulted for herself; her silence is consent. Laughter is stronger than silence; weeping is displeasure. A thayyib must speak.tap to reveal

Faṣl: Equality in status (kafāʾa)

Qudūrī states that kafāʾa counts; Hidāya gives the ḥadīth basis (Quraysh equal to each other, Arabs to Arabs, mawālī to mawālī) and the dispute over each criterion: nasab, dīn (AH/AY vs Muḥammad), wealth (mahr and nafaqa; AY only nafaqa; wealth-level AH/Muḥammad vs AY), and trades (AH two reports). It also adds the walī's objection to a below-parity dowry (AH vs the two) and the father's power to waive mahr or marry a minor to an unequal spouse (AH vs the two).
What is the proof that kafāʾa is considered, and why only on the husband's side?
ألا لا يزوج النساء إلا الأولياء ولا يزوجن إلا من الأكفاء ولأن انتظام المصالح بين المتكافئين عادة لأن الشريفة تأبى أن تكون مستفرشة للخسيس
Ḥadīth. A noble woman refuses to be the bed of a base man; the man is the mustafrish so her lowliness doesn't anger him.tap to reveal
How is lineage equality worked out?
قريش بعضهم أكفاء لبعض بطن بيطن والعرب بعضهم أكفاء لبعض قبيلة بقبيلة والموالي بعضهم أكفاء لبعض رجل برجل
Quraysh equal each other, Arabs each other, mawālī each other; no ranking inside Quraysh (Muḥammad: except famous house like the caliphal family). Bāhila not equal to general Arabs (known for lowliness).tap to reveal
Is religiosity (dīn) part of kafāʾa?
لأنه من أعلى المفاخر والمرأة تعير بفسق الزوج فوق ما تعير بضعة نسبه
AH and AY (ṣaḥīḥ): yes. Muḥammad: no, it concerns the akhira, unless the man is mocked, slapped, or goes drunk in markets.tap to reveal

Faṣl: Agency in nikāḥ and fuḍūlī

Qudūrī gave the cousin's and fuḍūlī rulings briefly. Hidāya adds the khilāf on whether one person may contract for both sides (Zufar and al-Shāfiʿī), why a nikāḥ agent is 'safīr' rather than the rightsholder, the doctrine of suspended contracts of the fuḍūlī (against al-Shāfiʿī), and the Abū Yūsuf dissent on a woman marrying herself to an absent man.
May a cousin, or a woman's agent, marry her to himself?
ولنا أن الوكيل في النكاح معبر وسفير والتمانع في الحقوق دون التعيير ولا ترجع الحقوق إليه بخلاف البيع لأنه مباشر حتى رجعت الحقوق إليه
Valid (Ḥanafīs), with two witnesses; Zufar and al-Shāfiʿī: not valid, as one person cannot be both mumallik and mutamallik (al-Shāfiʿī grants it only for a walī by necessity).tap to reveal
Is a nikāḥ contracted by a fuḍūlī valid when the party ratifies?
ولنا أن ركن التصرف صدر من أهله مضافا إلى محله ولا ضرر في انعقاده فينعقد موقوفا حتى إذا رأى المصلحة فيه ينفذه
Ḥanafīs: suspended and effective upon ratification; al-Shāfiʿī: all fuḍūlī transactions void since a contract is made for its effect which he cannot establish.tap to reveal
May one fuḍūlī act on both sides (e.g., a woman marries herself to an absent man who later ratifies)?
ولهما أن الموجود شطر العقد لأنه شطر حالة الحضرة فكذا عند الغيبة وشطر العقد لا يتوقف على ما وراء المجلس كما في البيع
AH and Muḥammad: no; AY: valid when she marries herself to an absent man who ratifies. If contract is between two fuḍūlīs or a fuḍūlī and an original party, valid by ijmāʿ.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Mahr (dowry)

Hidāya's Mahr chapter is where most of the khilāf lies. It grounds the minimum of ten dirhams (against al-Shāfiʿī and Mālik), distinguishes mahr as 'right of the Sharʿ' in its wujūb and 'her right' in its baqāʾ, then works through half-mahr, mutʿa, the khalwa ṣaḥīḥa and its disqualifying obstacles (illness, fasting, iḥrām, ḥayḍ; the majbūb), shighār, service as mahr, gifts-back-to-husband, conditional mahr, the 'one of two slaves' rule, unspecified genus, the fāsid marriage, mahr mithl criteria, guardian surety, her right to withhold herself, disputes about mahr and the 'gift or mahr' presumption.
What is the proof that the minimum mahr is ten dirhams?
ولنا قوله ﵊ ولا مهر أقل من عشرة ولأنه حق الشرع وجوبا إظهارا لشرف المحل فيتقدر بماله خطر وهو العشرة استدلالا بنصاب السرقة
Hadith "no mahr less than ten", plus reasoning: mahr is the Sharʿ's right displaying the importance of the woman, so it is measured by something of weight — ten, by analogy with the theft nisāb.tap to reveal
If less than ten is named, what is owed?
ولنا أن فساد هذه التسمية لحق الشرع وقد صار مقضيا بالعشرة فأما ما يرجع إلى حقها فقد رضيت بالعشرة لرضاها بما دونها
Ten dirhams (Ḥanafīs); Zufar: mahr mithl, as naming what is not valid is like no naming. If divorced before dukhūl: five (the three scholars); AH view quoted: mutʿa.tap to reveal
If no mahr was named and the husband died or consummated, what is owed?
ولنا أن المهر وجوبا حق الشرع على ما مر وإنما يصير حقا لها في حالة البقاء فتملك الإبراء دون النفي
Mahr mithl (Ḥanafīs). Shāfiʿī: nothing on death; most Shāfiʿīs: due on consummation. A divorced mufawwaḍa gets mutʿa (wājiba — Mālik: not obligatory).tap to reveal

Faṣl: Non-Muslims and mahr (carrion, wine, pork)

A short faṣl on marriages of non-Muslims. Hidāya adds the three-way khilāf about whether dhimmīs who named carrion or no mahr owe mahr (AH: no, because dhimmīs are not bound to our rulings in matters they believe permitted; the two: yes for dhimmīs, no for ḥarbīs; Zufar: yes for all), and the detailed rule if they marry on wine/pork and convert before receipt (AH/AY/Muḥammad three views).
Non-Muslim marries on carrion or with no mahr; what is owed?
ولأبي حنيفة ﵀ أن أهل الذمة لا يلتزمون أحكامنا في الديانات وفيما يعتقدون خلافه في المعاملات وولاية الإلزام بالسيف وبالمحاجة وكل ذلك منقطع عنهم باعتبار عقد الذمة
AH: no mahr for either ḥarbīs or dhimmīs. The two: ḥarbīs none; dhimmiyya gets mahr mithl (or mutʿa if divorced). Zufar: mahr mithl for ḥarbīs too (the law prescribed marriage only with property).tap to reveal

Bāb: Marriage of slaves

Hidāya on slave marriage grounds Qudūrī's rules in the master's authority and adds: Mālik's dissent (slave may marry on his own), the debtor-slave and mahr being an obligation on his neck, the master's ijbār (al-Shāfiʿī exempts the male slave), the killing of a slave bride before consummation (AH vs the two), ʿazl, khiyār al-ʿitq (Barīra hadith; Zufar on the mukātaba), a father using his son's slave woman, and the subtle case of freeing a slave-husband on behalf of his free wife.
May a slave marry without his master's permission?
ولأن في تنفيذ نكاحهما تعييبهما إذ النكاح عيب فيهما فلا يملكانه بدون إذن مولاهما
No — hadith: a slave who marries without his master's permission is an adulterer (ʿāhir). Mālik: the slave may, since he can pronounce ṭalāq. The mukātab, mudabbar and umm walad need permission too.tap to reveal
Whom does the mahr bind when a slave marries with permission?
والمدبر والمكاتب يسعيان في المهر ولا يباعان فيه لأنهما لا يحتملان النقل من ملك إلى ملك مع بقاء الكتابة والتدبير
It is a debt on his neck: he is sold for it. A mudabbar or mukātab works to pay it and is not sold.tap to reveal
May a master force his slave or slave woman into marriage?
ولنا أن الإنكاح إصلاح ملكه لأن فيه تحصينه عن الزنا الذي هو سبب الهلاك أو النقصان
Yes for both (Ḥanafīs); Shāfiʿī (and a narration of AH): not the male slave, as marriage is a human privilege. Not for mukātab/mukātaba (like free persons).tap to reveal

Why one year for the impotent man?
هكذا روي عن عمر وعلي وابن مسعود رضي الله تعالى عنهم ولأن الحق ثابت لها في الوطء ويحتمل أن يكون الامتناع لعلة معترضة ويحتمل لآفة أصلية فلا بد من مدة معرفة لذلك وقدرناها بالسنة لاشتمالها على الفصول الأربعة
