Kitāb al-Zakāh — conditions of obligation

Hidāya opens zakāh by proving the obligation and unpacking each condition in Qudūrī-style matn: freedom, sanity, puberty, Islam, niṣāb, full ownership, ḥawl. Its main khilāf is with al-Shāfiʿī who treats zakāh as a financial burden (so children and the indebted are charged) while Ḥanafīs treat it as an act of worship needing intent and free of basic needs. The section also covers ḍimār wealth, trade intention and niyya in payment.
Who must pay zakāh and what conditions apply?
الزكاة واجبة على الحر العاقل البالغ المسلم إذا ملك نصابا ملكا تاما وحال عليه الحول
Zakāh is obligatory (farḍ) on a free, sane, adult Muslim who owns a niṣāb in full ownership for a full year.tap to reveal
Is zakāh due on the wealth of a child or insane person?
وليس على الصبي والمجنون زكاة
Ḥanafīs: no. Shāfiʿī: yes, paid by the guardian.tap to reveal
Does a debt encompassing one's wealth cancel zakāh?
ومن كان عليه دين يحيط بماله فلا زكاة عليه
Yes, debt (owed to people) consumes the niṣāb; if wealth exceeds the debt, zakāh is due on the surplus if it reaches niṣāb.tap to reveal

A man has held 4,000 USD in savings for a lunar year, owes a 500 USD credit-card bill due now, and has a car and house for personal use. Is zakāh due?

The house/car are basic need and exempt. Immediate debt owed to people is deducted; the remainder, if at or above niṣāb (silver-based per many muftīs), is zakātable at 2.5%. Check the niṣāb with a muftī.discuss first — then tap

Bāb Ṣadaqat al-Sawāʾim (zakāh of livestock)

Livestock zakāh is described in tables from the Prophet's ﷺ letters: camels, cattle, sheep and goats, plus horses, which only Abū Ḥanīfa and Zufar tax. Hidāya's value is in the khilāf: the restart after 120 camels versus Shāfiʿī's 40/50 cycle; AH's proportional awqāṣ in cattle; what age sheep; infant animals; value in lieu of animals; and whether the ʿafw is taxed.
What is due on camels?
ليس في أقل من خمس ذود صدقة فإذا بلغت خمسا سائمة وحال عليها الحول ففيها شاة
5–9: 1 sheep; 10: 2; 15: 3; 20: 4; 25: bint makhāḍ; 36: bint labūn; 46: ḥiqqa; 61: jadhaʿa; 76: two bint labūn; 91: two ḥiqqa up to 120.tap to reveal
What after 120 camels?
وقال الشافعي ﵁ إذا زادت على مائة وعشرين واحدة ففيها ثلاث بنات لبون
Ḥanafī: the count restarts after 120 (5 more: add a sheep with two ḥiqqa; 150: three ḥiqqa; 196: four ḥiqqa). Shāfiʿī: at 121 it becomes three bint labūn, then 40/50 cycle.tap to reveal
What is due on cattle?
ليس في أقل من ثلاثين من البقر السائمة صدقة فإذا كانت ثلاثين سائمة وحال عليها الحول ففيها تبيع أو تبيعة
30: tabīʿ; 40: musinna; 60: two tabīʿ; 70: musinna + tabīʿ; every 10 swaps. Buffalo count as cattle.tap to reveal

Bāb Zakāt al-Māl (silver, gold, trade goods)

This bāb sets the niṣābs of silver and gold, the khilāf over kusūr (fractions), alloyed coin, jewellery, and trade goods. Hidāya adds proofs from the letters to Muʿādh and ʿAmr b. Ḥazm and the ʿaqlī grounds for pooling gold and silver. It is the base for modern savings, shares and business-stock questions.
What is the niṣāb of silver and the rate?
فإذا كانت مائتين وحال عليها الحول ففيها خمسة دراهم
200 dirhams = 5 dirhams (2.5%); gold: 20 mithqāl = half mithqāl.tap to reveal
Is zakāh due on the excess over 200 dirhams before 40?
ولا شيء في الزيادة حتى تبلغ أربعين درهما فيكون فيها درهم ثم في كل أربعين درهما درهم
AH: nothing until 40 more. Ṣāḥibayn and Shāfiʿī: proportionate.tap to reveal
What if coins are alloyed?
وإذا كان الغالب على الورق الفضة فهو في حكم الفضة وإذا كان الغالب عليها الغش فهو في حكم العروض
Majority silver = silver; majority alloy = goods, needing trade intent and value ≥ niṣāb.tap to reveal

A man holds shares in a listed company intending to trade them, with a market value above niṣāb, plus 1,000 in cash. What is zakāh based on?

Shares held for trade are ʿurūḍ tijāra: add market value + cash and pay 2.5% on the total if above niṣāb at ḥawl. Long-term investment shares are treated differently by contemporary muftīs; consult one.discuss first — then tap

Bāb man yamurru ʿalā al-ʿĀshir

The ʿāshir sits on the road and takes zakāh from traders who pass under the state's protection. Hidāya covers claims taken on oath, rates by status (Muslim, dhimmī, ḥarbī), the reciprocity rule, wine and pigs, and whose goods are taxed (muḍāraba, bidāʿa, authorised slave). Practical today as an analogy for customs and border taxes only.
Is the trader believed at the ʿāshir's post?
إذا مر على العاشر بمال فقال أصبته منذ أشهر أو علي دين وحلف صدق
Yes, with oath, if he claims the year has not passed or he has a debt, or has paid another ʿāshir.tap to reveal
Is a claim to have paid the poor himself accepted for goods passing the ʿāshir?
وفي الفصل الرابع: وهو ما إذا قال أديت بنفسي إلى الفقراء في المصر لا يصدق وإن حلف
Not for visible goods (sawāʾim, trade passing): not believed. Shāfiʿī: believed.tap to reveal
What rates apply to Muslims, dhimmīs and ḥarbīs?
ويؤخذ من المسلم ربع العشر ومن الذمي نصف العشر ومن الحربي العشر
Muslim 1/40; dhimmī 1/20; ḥarbī 1/10 (reciprocal; if unknown, 1/10 per ʿUmar).tap to reveal

Bāb al-Maʿādin wa al-Rikāz

Minerals and buried treasure are subject to a fifth. Ḥanafī reasoning: ghanīma. The khilāf covers a mine found in one's house, treasure in private land, mercury, pearls and ambergris; Shāfiʿī limits it to zakāh on extracted gold and silver.
What is due on mined minerals?
معدن ذهب أو فضة أو حديد أو رصاص أو صفر وجد في أرض خراج أو عشر ففيه الخمس
A fifth (khums). Shāfiʿī: nothing except zakāh on gold and silver extracted.tap to reveal
Is khums due on a mine found in one's own house?
ولو وجد في داره معدنا فليس فيه شيء
AH: none. Ṣāḥibayn: khums.tap to reveal
What of buried treasure?
وإن وجد ركازا ... وجب فيه الخمس
Khums if of Jāhilī stamp; if Islamic stamp, treat as luqaṭa. Four-fifths to the finder in free land.tap to reveal

Bāb Zakāt al-Zurūʿ wa al-Thimār (ʿushr)

ʿUshr on produce is the sharpest AH vs Ṣāḥibayn dispute in zakāh: AH imposes it on all produce with no niṣāb or lasting-fruit condition. Hidāya gives both sides' ḥadīth and AH's interpretation of the five-awsuq text as trade-zakāh. It also covers irrigation rates, honey, Taghlibī land and kharāj versus ʿushr water.
What is due on agricultural produce, and is there a niṣāb?
قال أبو حنيفة ﵀ في قليل ما أخرجته الأرض وكثيره العشر
AH: ʿushr on all produce, little or much, except firewood, reeds, grass. Ṣāḥibayn: only on lasting-fruit produce reaching five awsuq (a wasq = 60 ṣāʿ); none on vegetables.tap to reveal
What rate for irrigated land?
وما سقي بغرب أو دالية أو سانية ففيه نصف العشر على القولين
Half-ʿushr for irrigation by labour (bucket, wheel); full ʿushr for rain or natural flow; if mixed, the majority of the year.tap to reveal
Is there ʿushr on honey?
وفي العسل العشر إذا أخذ من أرض العشر
Yes (AH: little or much). Shāfiʿī: no, like silk.tap to reveal

Bāb the recipients of zakāh

Hidāya bases the recipients on Qurʾān 9:60 and discusses each category, the Shāfiʿī demand to cover all eight, and who is disqualified: dhimmīs, the wealthy, close relatives, Banū Hāshim. The Ḥanafī pillar is tamlīk, so no mosque, kafan, or debt of the dead. A key khilāf is what happens when one errs.
Who are the recipients of zakāh?
فهذه ثمانية أصناف وقد سقط منها المؤلفة قلوبهم
Eight categories (Qurʾān 9:60); the muʾallafa qulūbuhum category has lapsed since Allah strengthened Islam.tap to reveal
Must zakāh be divided among all categories (three of each)?
وقال الشافعي: لا يجوز أن يصرف إلى ثلاثة من كل صنف
No (Ḥanafī): one category, one person suffices. Shāfiʿī: all categories, at least three each.tap to reveal
May zakāh go to a dhimmī?
ولا يجوز أن يدفع الزكاة إلى ذمي
No; other ṣadaqa yes. Shāfiʿī: no.tap to reveal

Bāb Ṣadaqat al-Fiṭr

Fiṭra is wājib on a person owning a niṣāb surplus to needs. Hidāya explains whose fiṭra a man pays (those he maintains and holds authority over), the amounts and the ṣāʿ, and the time. The recurring Ḥanafī-Shāfiʿī gaps: threshold of wealth, who the debtor is (master vs slave), half-ṣāʿ wheat, the moment of obligation.
Who owes ṣadaqat al-fiṭr?
صدقة الفطر واجبة على الحر المسلم إذا كان مالكا لمقدار النصاب فاضلا عن مسكنه وثيابه وأثاثه وفرسه وسلاحه وعبيده
A free Muslim owning niṣāb surplus to basic needs (no growth required). Shāfiʿī: anyone with food beyond a day.tap to reveal
For whom does he pay?
يخرج ذلك عن نفسه
Himself, minor children, slaves for service; not his wife, adult children, mukātab, trade slaves.tap to reveal
Pay for a non-Muslim slave?
ويؤدي المسلم الفطر عبده الكافر
Yes; Shāfiʿī: no, since it is the slave's liability.tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Ṣawm — kinds of fasts and niyya

Hidāya opens fasting by dividing it into fixed-time fasts (Ramaḍān, specific vow) and fasts held in the dhimma (qaḍāʾ, kaffāra, absolute vow), then argues that fasting is a single extended pillar whose niyya may come before midday. Most of the debate is with al-Shāfiʿī, who requires a night niyya; Hidāya replies with the Bedouin's moon-sighting report and the argument from majority.
May Ramaḍān be fasted with a niyya made after dawn?
فيجوز بنية من الليل وإن لم ينو حتى أصبح أجزأته النية ما بينه وبين الزوال
Yes: for Ramaḍān and a fixed vow, niyya from the night or until before ḍaḥwa kubrā (midday, safer than zawāl). Shāfiʿī: not valid.tap to reveal
Does a mutlaq or nafl niyya fulfil Ramaḍān?
وهذا الضرب من الصوم يتأدى بمطلق النية وبنية النفل وبنية واجب آخر
Yes for a resident (Ḥanafī): the time is fixed so the general niyya hits it. Ṣāḥibayn: same for traveller/sick. AH: traveller with another wājib niyya counts as that.tap to reveal
By when must a nafl or non-fixed fast be intended?
والنفل كله يجوز بنية قبل الزوال
Nafl: before zawāl (mālik: not valid after). Qaḍāʾ, kaffāra, absolute vow: only from the night. Shāfiʿī: even after zawāl for nafl.tap to reveal

Faṣl — sighting the crescent

The faṣl on ruʾyat al-hilāl: complete 30 days when clouded, the five forms of the day of doubt, lone sighting, and the standards of testimony in cloud and clear sky (one witness vs a large body), with a different rule for the Eid crescent. It ends with the definition of fasting and its time.
What if the sky is cloudy on the 29th of Shaʿbān?
وإن غم عليهم أكملوا عدة شعبان ثلاثين يوما ثم صاموا
Complete Shaʿbān as 30.tap to reveal
May one fast the day of doubt?
ولا يصومون يوم الشك إلا تطوعا
Only as nafl. Five types: intend Ramaḍān (makrūh); another wājib (makrūh, lesser); nafl (not makrūh); wavering in the root niyya (not a fast); wavering in description (makrūh).tap to reveal
If one alone sights Ramaḍān's moon and the judge rejects?
ومن رأى هلال رمضان وحده صام وإن لم يقبل الإمام شهادته
He fasts. If he breaks it: qaḍāʾ but no kaffāra. Shāfiʿī: kaffāra if by intercourse.tap to reveal

A local committee receives a single report of sighting from a trustworthy man in a cloudy evening.

Cloudy: one adult trustworthy report is accepted for Ramaḍān start per Ḥanafī rule; clear sky requires a large group. Eid needs two. Follow local muftīs.discuss first — then tap

Bāb al-Ḥajj ʿan al-Ghayr (proxy Ḥajj)

The principle: one may dedicate the reward of any act to another, but proxy performance applies only to financial acts always, and to mixed acts such as Ḥajj on permanent inability. Can one transfer the reward of an act to another?

Yes (Ahl al-Sunna), whether prayer, fast, charity or Ḥajj.tap to reveal

ثم ظاهر المذهب أن الحج يقع عن المحجوج عنه
