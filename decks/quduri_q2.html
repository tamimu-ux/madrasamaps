Book of Ijara (Lease and Hire)

Ijara is the sale of benefits for a return. The Qudūrī first sets the condition of knowing both benefit and wage, then goes through housing, land, animals, and workers; the long discussion of the shared worker versus the exclusive worker is the heart of the chapter. It ends with when the wage is due and what dissolves the lease.
What is ijara?
الإجارة: عقد على المنافع بعوض
Ijara is a contract that exchanges benefits for a return.tap to reveal
What must be known for ijara to be valid?
ولا تصح حتى تكون المنافع معلومة والأجرة معلومة وما جاز أن يكون ثمنا في البيع جاز أن يكون أجرة في الإجارة
Both the benefit and the wage must be known; whatever can be a price in sale can be a wage.tap to reveal
How does a benefit become known?
والمنافع تارة تصير معلومة بالمدة كاستئجار الدور للسكنى والأرضين للزراعة
By time period (houses, land), by work and specification (dyeing, sewing, carrying a stated load), or by pointing out the specific item.tap to reveal
1 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

A tenant rents a flat for a year. After six months he loses his job and wants to leave.

The book names a trader whose capital is lost as an excuse; losing a salaried job is only an analogy, so a judge or the landlord would need to agree to rescind. Without that, the rent stays due (rule wajib: wage is earned as the benefit is delivered).discuss first — then tap

Book of Shufa (Pre-emption)

Shufa lets a co-owner or neighbour take over a sold property at the buyer's price in order to prevent harm from a new neighbour. The Qudūrī gives the order of claimants, the two witnessed demands, what voids the right, and how the price and any buildings are treated.
Who is entitled to shufa, and in what order?
الشفعة: واجبة للخيلط في نفس المبيع ثم للخليط في حق المبيع كالشرب والطريق ثم للجار
Co-owner in the thing itself; then the co-owner in a right of the thing (water, road); then the neighbour.tap to reveal
Does a lower-ranking claimant compete with a higher one?
وليس للشريك في الطريق والشرب والجار شفعة مع الخليط فإن سلم فالشفعة للشريك في الطريق فإن سلم أخذها الجار
No; only if the higher one waives does the next take it.tap to reveal
When does the right arise, settle, and become ownership?
والشفعة تجب بعقد البيع وتستقر بالإشهاد وتملك بالأخذ إذا سلمها المشتري أو حكم بها حاكم
It arises with the sale, becomes firm with the witnessing, and ownership transfers upon taking, by buyer's surrender or a judge's ruling.tap to reveal
2 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

My neighbour sold his flat to a stranger. I own the adjoining unit. Do I have a claim?

Under Hanafi law as in the Qudūrī: the neighbour's right is third rank; he must witness the demand immediately and then again. Failing to do so voids it.discuss first — then tap

Book of Shirka (Partnership)

Partnership has two kinds: co-ownership and contract. Contract partnership splits into mufawada, ʿinān, crafts and credit. The Qudūrī lays down what counts as capital, how profit must be fixed (as a ratio, never a sum), and what ends a partnership.
What are the two kinds of shirka?
الشركة على ضربين: شركة أملاك وشركة عقود
Ownership partnership and contract partnership.tap to reveal
Two people inherit or buy a house together; can one deal in the other's share?
فشركة الأملاك: العين يرثها رجلان أو يشتريانها فلا يجوز لأحدهما أن ينصرف في نصيب الآخر إلا بإذنه وكل واحد منهما في نصيب صاحبه كالأجنبي
No, not without permission; each is a stranger in the other's share.tap to reveal
How many contract partnerships?
وهي على أربعة أوجه: مفاوضة وعنان وشركة الصنائع وشركة الوجوه
Four: mufawada, ʿinān, crafts, credit.tap to reveal
3 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

Two friends open a cafe. A puts in $20,000, B puts in nothing but works full-time. They agree profits split 50/50.

This is an ʿinān-type arrangement; unequal capital and equal profit is allowed because B contributes work.discuss first — then tap

Book of Mudaraba (Profit-Sharing Partnership)

Mudaraba joins one man's capital to another's labour, with the profit shared as a ratio. The Qudūrī sets the validity conditions, the mudarib's powers under restricted and unrestricted terms, the question of sub-mudaraba, and what happens on dismissal, death, and loss.
What is mudaraba?
المضاربة: عقد على الشركة بمال من أحد الشريكين وعمل من الآخر
A partnership where capital comes from one side and work from the other.tap to reveal
What may be capital?
ولا تصح المضاربة إلا بالمال الذي بيننا أن الشركة تصح به
Only what is valid capital in shirka (dirhams, dinars, current coin).tap to reveal
How must the profit be set?
ومن شرطها: أن يكون الربح بينهما مشاعا لا يستحق أحدهما منه دراهم مسماة
As a shared ratio; neither may be given a fixed sum.tap to reveal
4 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

Ali gives $50,000 to Omar to trade, profit 60/40. Omar says he'll guarantee Ali's capital no matter what.

Capital is trust; the mudarib does not guarantee loss. A promised guarantee conflicts with the Qudūrī's principle; study aid only.discuss first — then tap

Book of Wakala (Agency)

Agency lets one act through another in whatever one may do himself. The Qudūrī distinguishes agents who bear the rights of the contract from those who are mere messengers, limits agents' freedom in price and in appointing others, and lists what terminates the agency.
What may be delegated?
كل عقد جاز أن يعقده الإنسان بنفسه جاز أن يوكل به غيره
Any contract one may make himself, he may delegate.tap to reveal
Litigation and collection.
ويجوز التوكيل بالخصومة في سائر الحقوق وبإثباتها ويجوز التوكيل بالاستيفاء إلا في الحدود والقصاص فإن الوكالة لا تصح باستيفائهما مع غيبة الموكل عن المجلس
Agency valid in litigation of all rights and proving them; and in collecting, except hudud and qisas when the principal is absent.tap to reveal
Is the adversary's consent needed to appoint a litigation agent?
وقال أبو حنيفة: لا يجوز التوكيل بالخصومة إلا برضا الخصم إلا أن يكون الموكل مريضا أو غائبا مسيرة ثلاثة أيام فصاعدا
Abu Hanifa: not valid without the opponent's consent, unless the principal is sick or absent three days' travel or more.tap to reveal
5 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

Sara asks her brother to buy a used car for 'around $8,000'. He pays $12,000.

A mandate with a price expects a fair purchase; a price outside the tolerable range does not bind the principal.discuss first — then tap

Book of Kafala (Suretyship)

Kafala is of the person (producing the debtor) or of money. The Qudūrī covers wording, delivery, and discharge for the first, and for the second what can be guaranteed, the creditor's choice of whom to claim from, and the surety's right of recourse.
Kinds of kafala.
الكفالة ضربان: كفالة بالنفس وكفالة بالمال
Surety for the person, and surety for money.tap to reveal
What does the person-surety guarantee?
فالكفالة بالنفس جائزة والمضمون بها إحضار المكفول به
Valid; what is guaranteed is producing him.tap to reveal
Fixed time of production.
فإن شرط في الكفالة تسليم المكفول به في وقت بعينه لزمه إحضاره إذا طالبه به في ذلك الوقت فإن أحضره وإلا حبسه الحاكم حتى يحضره
He must produce him then; else the judge imprisons him until he does.tap to reveal

The creditor may claim from either. If Hamza guaranteed at his friend's request, he recovers what he pays.discuss first — then tap

Book of Hawala (Transfer of Debt)

Hawala moves a debt from one liability to another with everyone's consent. The transferor is discharged unless the creditor's right is lost, called tawa. The short chapter, which breaks off mid-dispute in this edition, is a good contrast with kafala.
What may be transferred and who must agree?
الحوالة: جائزة بالديون وتصح برضا المحيل والمحتال له والمحال عليه
Debts; with the consent of the transferor, the creditor and the transferee.tap to reveal
Effect of the completed transfer.
وإذا تمت الحوالة برىء المحيل من الدين ولم يرجع المحتال على المحيل إلاأن يتوى حقه
The transferor is discharged; the creditor has no recourse unless his right is lost (tawa).tap to reveal
When is the right lost (tawa)?
والتوى عند أبي حنيفة أحد أمرين: إما أن يجحد الحوالة ويحلف ولا بينة عليه أو يموت مفلسا
Denial of the transfer on oath without proof, or death in poverty (Abu Hanifa).tap to reveal

Hawala: all three agree, debt moves, and the shop is discharged.discuss first — then tap

Book of Sulh (Settlement)

Sulh ends a dispute by agreement. The Qudūrī distinguishes settlement after admission (like a sale or hire) from settlement upon denial or silence (ransom for the defendant, exchange for the claimant), then sets what cannot be settled and how settlement of debts and of inheritance shares is shaped to avoid riba.
What kinds of sulh are there?
الصلح: على ثلاثة أضرب: صلح مع إقرار وصلح مع سكوت وهو أن لا يقر المدعى عليه ولا ينكره وصلح مع إنكار وكل ذلك جائز
Settlement with admission, with silence, and with denial; all are valid.tap to reveal
Rulings on settlement after admission.
فإن وقع الصلح عن إقرار اعتبر فيه ما يعتبر في البياعات إن وقع عن مال بمال وإن وقع عن مال بمنافع فيعتبر بالإجارات
Treated as sale if money for money; as hire if for benefits.tap to reveal
Nature of settlement upon denial or silence.
والصلح عن السكوت والإنكار في حق المدعى عليه لافتداء اليمين وقطع الخصومة وفي حق المدعي بمعنى المعاوضة
For the defendant: ransoming the oath and cutting off the dispute; for the claimant: an exchange.tap to reveal
8 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

A neighbour's tree damages your car. He denies fault but offers $1,000 to settle a $3,000 claim.

Valid settlement upon denial: ransom for him, exchange for you.discuss first — then tap

Book of Hiba (Gift)

Hiba is a gratuitous transfer of ownership, formed by offer and acceptance and completed by possession. The Qudūrī treats undivided shares, gifts to minors, retraction and its bars, gifts for a return, umra and ruqba, and sadaqa, which cannot be retracted.
How is a gift formed?
الهبة: تصح بالإيجاب والقبول
By offer and acceptance, and it is completed by possession.tap to reveal
Possession in the sitting or after.
فإذا قبض الموهوب له في المجلس بغير أمر الواهب جاز
Valid if he takes it in the sitting without the donor's command.tap to reveal
Gift of a divisible thing.
ولا تجوز الهبة فيما يقسم إلا محوزة مقسومة
Valid only if separated and divided.tap to reveal

Maryam gives her friend a car but regrets it a month later. The friend refuses to return it.

A stranger: she may retract, but only by consent or a judge, unless a bar exists.discuss first — then tap

Quick quiz

Answer aloud, then tap the row

1Death of the pre-emptor / the buyer.
The right ends if the claimant dies, but not if the buyer dies.
2The buyer built or planted before ruling.
The claimant chooses: take it with the building value (as if removed) or compel the buyer to remove it.
3When is the right lost (tawa)?
Denial of the transfer on oath without proof, or death in poverty (Abu Hanifa).
4The two companions.
The same two plus a judicial declaration of insolvency in his lifetime.
5What may be delegated?
Any contract one may make himself, he may delegate.
6And where use does not depend on the user?
No liability; e.g. if one occupant is stipulated, he may house another.
7Contracts the agent attributes to himself.
Rights attach to the agent, not the principal.
8Who is entitled to shufa, and in what order?
Co-owner in the thing itself; then the co-owner in a right of the thing (water, road); then the neighbour.
9How does a benefit become known?
By time period (houses, land), by work and specification (dyeing, sewing, carrying a stated load), or by pointing out the specific item.
10When may a launderer/tailor demand wages?
Only after finishing the work, unless advance payment was stipulated.
