Kitāb al-Shirka — mufāwaḍa & ʿinān

Hidāya's shirka chapter adds the reasoning behind Qudūrī's four kinds. The main disputes: why mufāwaḍa is allowed only by istiḥsān, whether it can be formed between a Muslim and a dhimmī (AH/Muḥammad vs Abū Yūsuf), whether suretyship for a stranger binds the partner (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn), and the big Shāfiʿī/Zufar objection that profit may not differ from capital share and that mixing (khalṭ) of capital is required. Hidāya answers with the ḥadīth 'profit follows the stipulation, loss follows the two capitals' and with the principle that profit arises from taṣarruf (agency+principalship), not from the property itself.
Why is mufāwaḍa valid only by istiḥsān, and who rejects it?
وجه القياس أنها تضمنت الوكالة بمجهول الجنس والكفالة بمجهول، وكل ذلك بانفراده فاسد
By qiyās it is invalid (agency for an unknown genus + suretyship for unknown amounts). Ḥanafīs allow it by istiḥsān; Shāfiʿī rejects it; Mālik said "I do not know what mufāwaḍa is".tap to reveal
What is the textual basis for allowing mufāwaḍa against qiyās?
وجه الاستحسان قوله ﷺ "فاوضوا فإنه أعظم للبركة"
The Prophet said: fāwiḍū, for it is greater in blessing; plus people dealt in it without objection.tap to reveal
Must the word "mufāwaḍa" be used?
ولا تنعقد إلا بلفظة المفاوضة" لبعد شرائطها عن علم العوام، حتى لو بينا جميع ما تقتضيه تجوز لأن المعتبر هو المعنى
The contract is concluded only by the word mufāwaḍa, because its conditions are remote from laymen's knowledge; but if the parties spell out all its requirements it is valid since meaning governs.tap to reveal

Shirka — ṣanāʾiʿ, wujūh, invalidity, ending

The second half of shirka adds Hidāya's reasoning on the labour- and credit-partnerships. Zufar and Shāfiʿī reject both because they have no capital; the Ḥanafīs say profit arises from taṣarruf (each partner is agent for the other) and, in ṣanāʾiʿ, from guarantee of work. Hidāya explains why unequal work shares are allowed in ṣanāʾiʿ by istiḥsān but unequal profit in wujūh is not, and adds details on hunting, the death of a partner and zakāh.
Is a labour partnership (ṣanāʾiʿ) valid?
ولنا أن المقصود منه التحصيل وهو ممكن بالتوكيل، لأنه لما كان وكيلا في النصف أصيلا في النصف تحققت الشركة في المال المستفاد
Valid (Ḥanafīs). Zufar and Shāfiʿī: invalid, as it does not give tathmīr (growth), which needs capital.tap to reveal
If work is shared equally but profit/ownership is 2:1, is it valid?
ولو شرطا العمل نصفين والمال أثلاثا جاز" وفي القياس: لا يجوز
Valid by istiḥsān; qiyās: invalid, since guarantee follows work.tap to reveal
Who is liable for work accepted by one ṣanāʾiʿ partner?
وما يتقبله كل واحد منهما من العمل يلزمه ويلزم شريكه" حتى إن كل واحد منهما يطالب بالعمل ويطالب بالأجر
Both are bound; each may be asked for the work and demand the fee; the payer is discharged by paying either. Obvious in mufāwaḍa; elsewhere it is istiḥsān (qiyās otherwise).tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Waqf

Qudūrī gave the three positions on when the founder loses ownership; Hidāya supplies the definitions, proofs and the many branching masāʾil. AH: waqf is ḥabs of the thing on the founder's ownership with charity of its usufruct, like ʿāriya, so it is not binding unless a judge rules or it is tied to death. The Ṣāḥibayn: ownership passes to Allah so it becomes binding (ḥadīth of ʿUmar's Thamgh). Hidāya then covers mushāʿ, perpetuity, movables (Muḥammad's istiḥsān by custom), the founder's own benefit and trusteeship, and how mosques, wells, inns and graveyards differ.
How do AH and the Ṣāḥibayn define waqf?
وهو في الشرع عند أبي حنيفة: حبس العين على ملك الواقف والتصدق بالمنفعة بمنزلة العارية
AH: ḥabs on the founder's ownership + charity of the benefit; non-binding like ʿāriya (the soundest view: valid but not binding, not void). Ṣāḥibayn: ḥabs on Allah's ownership; binding; cannot be sold, given or inherited.tap to reveal
What is the Ṣāḥibayn's evidence that waqf is binding?
تصدق بأصلها لا يباع ولا يورث ولا يوهب
The Prophet to ʿUmar about his land Thamgh: give its principal in charity, not to be sold, inherited or given. Reason: need for continuing reward; a precedent exists in the mosque.tap to reveal
What is AH's evidence?
قوله ﵊: "لا حبس عن فرائض الله تعالى"
The Prophet: no ḥabs (tying up) from Allah's fixed shares; from Shurayḥ "Muḥammad came with the sale of the ḥabīs".tap to reveal

Kitāb al-Buyūʿ — conclusion, price, quantity, appurtenances

Hidāya's opening of the sales chapter adds the Shāfiʿī dispute on khiyār al-majlis, the proof for deferred-price sales, the Abū Ḥanīfa/Ṣāḥibayn dispute on 'each qafīz for a dirham', the long family of disputes about shortfall/surplus in sale by measure vs by description (dhirāʿ as waṣf or as miqdār), and what is included in the sale of a house or tree. Everything Qudūrī merely states is left as inherited nodes.
Is there khiyār al-majlis after offer and acceptance?
ولنا أن في الفسخ إبطال حق الآخر فلا يجوز. والحديث محمول على خيار القبول
No (Ḥanafīs): after offer+acceptance sale is binding, only defect or non-sight gives an option. Shāfiʿī: each has an option until they part, by "al-mutabāyiʿān bil-khiyār mā lam yatafarraqā".tap to reveal
What is the Shāfiʿī evidence?
لقوله ﵊: "المتبايعان بالخيار ما لم يتفرقا"
The ḥadīth: the two parties to a sale have the choice as long as they have not separated.tap to reveal
Is a sale concluded by taking and giving without words?
ولهذا ينعقد بالتعاطي في النفيس والخسيس هو الصحيح لتحقق المراضاة
Yes, in both cheap and valuable goods (correct view) since consent is realised; also words like raḍītu, aʿṭaytuka, khudh-hu mean bayʿ.tap to reveal

Bāb Khiyār al-Sharṭ

Hidāya adds the proof (Ḥabbān b. Munqidh), the three-day limit dispute (AH vs Ṣāḥibayn), and above all who owns the object during the option: for AH the buyer does not own it, for the Ṣāḥibayn he does. A whole family of 'sisters' (buying one's wife, a relative, istibrāʾ, umm walad) depends on that single principle. It also gives the istiḥsān behind an option for a third party and for choosing among two or three items.
What is the text for khiyār al-sharṭ?
إذا بايعت فقل لا خلابة ولي الخيار ثلاثة أيام
The Prophet told Ḥabbān b. Munqidh, who was often cheated: say "no deception, and I have the option for three days".tap to reveal
May the option exceed three days?
ولأبي حنيفة أن شرط الخيار يخالف مقتضى العقد وهو اللزوم، وإنما جوزناه بخلاف القياس لما رويناه من النص، فيقتصر على المدة المذكورة فيه
AH, Zufar, Shāfiʿī: no. Ṣāḥibayn: yes if the period is known (ḥadīth of Ibn ʿUmar allowing two months; need for deliberation, like deferred price).tap to reveal
"If I do not pay the price within three days there is no sale"?
ولو اشترى على أنه إن لم ينقد الثمن إلى ثلاثة أيام فلا بيع بينهما جاز. وإلى أربعة أيام لا يجوز عند أبي حنيفة وأبي يوسف. وقال محمد: يجوز إلى أربعة أيام أو أكثر
Valid for three days. Four: invalid (AH, AY), valid (Muḥammad). It is attached to the option. Zufar: a sale with an invalid iqāla.tap to reveal

Bāb Khiyār al-Ruʾya

Hidāya adds the proof for buying the unseen against Shāfiʿī, the reason AH retracted the seller's option, the Companions' arbitration (ʿUthmān/Ṭalḥa), and long details of what suffices as sight (face of heap, façade of cloth, house courtyard). It also explains why the agent-for-receipt and the messenger differ.
What proves buying the unseen?
من اشترى شيئا لم يره فله الخيار إذا رآه
The ḥadīth: whoever buys what he has not seen has the option on seeing it; ignorance leads to no dispute since he may return it. Shāfiʿī: void due to uncertainty.tap to reveal
Does a seller of the unseen get the option?
ووجه القول المرجوع إليه أنه معلق بالشراء لما روينا فلا يثبت دونه
Now no (AH retracted his first opinion that it is like defect/sharṭ). Evidence: ʿUthmān sold land in Baṣra to Ṭalḥa; each claimed an option; Jubayr b. Muṭʿim ruled for Ṭalḥa, before the Companions.tap to reveal
How long does khiyār al-ruʾya last and what ends it?
ثم خيار الرؤية غير مؤقت بل يبقى إلى أن يوجد ما يبطله
It is not timed; it ends by what ends the option of sharṭ: defect or an act. Irreversible acts or acts creating others' rights (manumission, tadbīr, sale, pledge, lease) end it before and after sight; reversible ones (sale with option, haggling, ungiven gift) end it only after sight.tap to reveal

Bāb Khiyār al-ʿAyb

Hidāya shows why the option for defect exists (the contract implies soundness), why price-deduction (arsh) is not available while return is possible, and then works out when return becomes impossible and the buyer gets only the deduction (naqṣān). The key khilāf: consuming, killing or wearing out the item (AH: no recourse; Ṣāḥibayn: recourse), a slave who had stolen (AH: like istiḥqāq, return; Ṣāḥibayn: like defect, deduction), the two-buyer and partial-possession cases, and Shāfiʿī's rejection of the 'barāʾa from every defect' clause.
May the buyer keep the item and take the price deduction?
وليس له أن يمسكه ويأخذ النقصان" لأن الأوصاف لا يقابلها شيء من الثمن في مجرد العقد
No. Qualities take no share of the price in the bare contract, and the seller did not agree to part with it for less; harm to buyer is cured by return.tap to reveal
What is a defect?
وكل ما أوجب نقصان الثمن في عادة التجار فهو عيب
Whatever lowers the price in merchants' custom; reference is to experts.tap to reveal
Running away, bedwetting, stealing in a minor?
والإباق والبول في الفراش والسرقة في الصغير عيب ما لم يبلغ، فإذا بلغ فليس ذلك بعيب حتى يعاوده بعد البلوغ
Defect until puberty; after puberty it is a new defect unless it recurs. Insanity as a child is a defect forever. Bad breath: only in slave-girls; fornication: slave-girls (slaves only if habitual); unbelief: both.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Bayʿ al-Fāsid — prohibited objects, gharar, conditions

Qudūrī lists the invalid sales; Hidāya classifies them. Its key concept is the difference between bāṭil (the pillar, exchange of māl for māl, is missing: carrion, blood, a free person) and fāsid (the pillar exists but a description is faulty: wine/pig in barter). It then supplies the texts for each prohibition (gharar, milk in udder, muzābana, mulāmasa), the Muḥammad/AH/AY splits on bees, silkworms, eggs and the absconding slave, and the general rule on conditions: a condition that the contract does not require and that benefits a party or the object invalidates the sale, unless custom makes it normal.
What is the difference between a void and a corrupt sale?
والبيع بالخمر والخنزير فاسد لوجود حقيقة البيع وهو مبادلة المال بالمال فإنه مال عند البعض والباطل لا يفيد ملك التصرف
Carrion/blood/free person: bāṭil (not property for anyone, no pillar, no ownership). Wine/pig as price: if against debts (dirhams) bāṭil; against a specified item it is fāsid and the other item is owned (its value is due), since the aim is to own the cloth, not to honour the wine.tap to reveal
Umm walad / mudabbar dies with the buyer of a void sale?
وله أن جهة البيع إنما تلحق بحقيقة في محل يقبل الحقيقة وهما لا يقبلان حقيقة البيع فصارا كالمكاتب
AH: no liability. Ṣāḥibayn: their value (seized under pretext of sale). Reason AH: they cannot be truly sold, like a mukātab; entering the sale only to extend to what is joined.tap to reveal

Bāb al-Ribā

Qudūrī gives the rulings; Hidāya gives the ʿilla debate. Ḥanafīs: ribā al-faḍl arises from qadr (kayl or wazn) together with jins; Shāfiʿī: ṭuʿm in foods and thamaniyya in currencies, with jins a condition. Kitāb al-Ṣarf

Hidāya supplies the etymologies, the proof for hand-to-hand exchange (ʿUmar, Ibn ʿUmar), and works out the sharper masāʾil Qudūrī only states: invalidity of disposal of the price before possession, whether the pillar divides when two currencies mix (Zufar and Shāfiʿī reject distributive allocation), the doctrine of 'distribution to the opposite genus' for validity, how debts are set off, and the long fatwā-level discussion of base coin and kasād of currency (AH: sale void; AY: value on day of sale; Muḥammad: value on last day of circulation).
Why is it called ṣarf?
سمي به للحاجة إلى النقل في بدليه من يد إلى يد. والصرف هو النقل والرد لغة، أو لأنه لا يطلب منه إلا الزيادة إذ لا ينتفع بعينه
For the need to transfer both counter-values from hand to hand (ṣarf = transfer/return), or because only surplus is sought as the metal itself is not used (ṣarf = increase, Khalīl; nāfila is also called ṣarf).tap to reveal

وإن استنظرك أن يدخل بيته فلا تنظره

Bodily parting is meant: walking together or sleeping in the meeting does not void (Ibn ʿUmar: if he jumps from the roof jump with him).tap to reveal
Option or term in ṣarf?
ولهذا لا يصح شرط الخيار فيه ولا الأجل لأن بأحدهما لا يبقى القبض مستحقا وبالثاني يفوت القبض المستحق

Kitāb al-Ḥawāla

Hidāya grounds testimony on testimony in need (istiḥsān), rejects it in ḥudūd, argues two witnesses may transmit two originals (against Shāfiʿī's four), details wording and the conditions of inability of the originals (death, three days' travel, illness), identification by lineage, and ends with the punishment of the false witness: tashhīr (AH) vs beating and prison (Ṣāḥibayn).

وهذا استحسان لشدة الحاجة إليها، إذ شاهد الأصل قد يعجز عن أداء الشهادة لبعض العوارض، فلو لم تجز الشهادة على الشهادة أدى إلى إتواء الحقوق
By istiḥsān for need (an original witness may be unable); doubt of substitution and extra possibility of error, avoided by number, bars it in what is averted by doubt (ḥudūd, qiṣāṣ).tap to reveal
Two witnesses transmitting two originals?

Kitāb al-Rujūʿ ʿan al-Shahāda

Qudūrī gave the amounts; Hidāya gives the logic. Retraction before judgment voids the testimony with no liability; after judgment the ruling stands but the witnesses pay what they destroyed (tasabbub like the digger of a well), against Shāfiʿī who denies liability. The principle is 'those who remain, not those who retract' (liability falls only if the remaining witnesses no longer support the whole right). Key khilāf: the man and ten women (AH sixths vs Ṣāḥibayn half/half), retracting qiṣāṣ witnesses (Shāfiʿī: qiṣāṣ), retracting branches vs originals (Muḥammad vs AH/AY) and tazkiya (AH liable).
Retraction before the ruling?
لأن الحق إنما يثبت بالقضاء والقاضي لا يقضي بكلام متناقض ولا ضمان عليهما لأنهما ما أتلفا شيئا
Testimony falls: the right is established by judgment and the judge does not rule on contradictory speech; no liability as nothing was destroyed.tap to reveal
Retraction after the ruling?
لأن آخر كلامهم يناقض أوله فلا ينقض الحكم بالتناقض ولأنه في الدلالة على الصدق مثل الأول، وقد ترجح الأول باتصال القضاء به
Ruling not reversed (later speech contradicts the earlier and has equal value, the first strengthened by the judgment); they pay what they destroyed: their admission is binding, contradiction does not nullify an admission.tap to reveal
Where must retraction take place?
ولأن الرجوع توبة والتوبة على حسب الجناية، فالسر بالسر والإعلان بالإعلان
Only before a judge (any judge), as it undoes testimony tied to court; also tawba matches the sin. Outside court claims of retraction are not sworn on or proved; proof that they retracted before judge X and he held them liable is accepted.tap to reveal
