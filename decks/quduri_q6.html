Kitab al-Dawa (Claims)

This kitāb teaches how a dispute is processed in court: who is claimant and defendant, what makes a claim admissible, the progression from admission to proof to oath to refusal (nukūl). It then handles the harder cases: conflicting proofs (two buyers, holder against outsider, birth proofs), mutual oaths when buyer and seller disagree, dower disputes, household goods, and paternity claims on a sold slave-girl. The big idea: proof is the claimant's, the oath the denier's, and possession (yad) gives priority when proofs are equal. It sets the groundwork for the next kitāb, Shahādāt.
What if the defendant refuses to swear?
وإذا نكل المدعى عليه عن اليمين قضي عليه بالنكول ولزمه ما ادعى عليه
Judgment is passed against him for refusal and he owes what is claimed. The judge should say: 'I offer you the oath three times; if you swear (well), otherwise I judge against you'; after three offers he rules by nukūl.tap to reveal
Is the oath returned to the claimant?
ولا ترد اليمين على المدعي
No. The oath is not returned to the claimant.tap to reveal
If the claimant says 'I have proof' and asks for the oath, is the defendant sworn?
فإن قال: لي بينة وطلب اليمين لم يستحلف عند أبي حنيفة
According to Abū Ḥanīfa, he is not sworn at that point: the claimant must first produce proof.tap to reveal
1 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

Sara says her brother Omar owes her $5,000 from a loan. Omar denies it. She has no receipts or witnesses but wants the court to ask him to swear.

Sara is the claimant and must state kind and amount (da_shart). With no proof she asks for the oath (da_procedure). If Omar refuses to swear after three offers, judgment is passed against him by nukūl (da_nukul); Shāfiʿī would return the oath to Sara. Study aid, not a fatwa.discuss first — then tap

Kitab al-Shahadat (Testimony)

Testimony is the main proof in court. This kitāb states the duty to testify, the nisāb for different matters (four men for zinā; two men for other ḥudūd/qiṣāṣ; two men or man and two women for rights; one woman for matters only women see), the conditions (uprightness, word 'ashhad'), who is accepted or rejected, how testimony must match the claim, hearsay testimony in five matters, testimony on testimony, and the penalty of false witnesses. It connects forward to the bāb on retraction.
Is giving testimony obligatory?
فرض يلزم الشهود أداؤها ولا يسعهم كتمانها إذا طالبهم المدعي
Yes: it is a duty upon the witnesses; they may not conceal it once the claimant asks.tap to reveal
What of testimony in ḥudūd?
والشهادة في الحدود يخير فيها الشاهد بين الستر والإظهار والستر أفضل إلا أنه يجب أن يشهد بالمال في السرقة فيقول: أخذ ولا يقول: سرق
The witness may choose between concealing and revealing; concealing is better. In theft he must testify for the property, saying 'he took' not 'he stole', so the amount is protected without the hadd.tap to reveal
How many witnesses for zinā and other hudud?
والشهادة على مراتب: منها الشهادة في الزنا يعتبر فيها أربعة من الرجال ولا تقبل فيها شهادة النساء ومنها الشهادة ببقية الحدود والقصاص تقبل فيها شهادة رجلين ولا تقبل فيها شهادة النساء
Zinā: four men; no women. Other ḥudūd and qiṣāṣ: two men; no women.tap to reveal
2 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

A neighbour saw someone take a laptop from a shared office. The owner asks him to testify, but he is not sure whether to report or stay quiet.

In ḥudūd cases covering is preferable, but he must testify to the property, saying 'took' (sh_hudud). Once the claimant demands it, the duty applies (sh_adaa).discuss first — then tap

Bab al-Ruju an al-Shahada (Retraction)

When witnesses retract, the effect depends on timing: before judgment the testimony falls; after judgment the ruling stands but the witnesses owe compensation for what they destroyed. Retraction is valid only before the judge. The bāb works out shares: two witnesses (all/half), three (none until a second leaves), man and women (quarter/half), and special cases of dower, sale, divorce, manumission, qiṣāṣ, root/branch, iḥṣān and oath.
What happens if witnesses retract?
إذا رجع الشهود عن شهادتهم قبل الحكم بها سقطت وإن حكم بشهادتهم ثم رجعوا لم يفسخ الحكم ووجب عليهم ضمان ما أتلفوه بشهادتهم ولا يصح الرجوع إلا بحضرة الحاكم
Before judgment, testimony falls. After judgment, the ruling is not annulled but they owe compensation for what they destroyed. Retraction is valid only in the judge's presence.tap to reveal
Share of liability when two or three retract?
وإذا شهد شاهدان بمال فحكم الحاكم به ثم رجعا ضمنا المال المشهود عليه وإن رجع أحدهما ضمن النصف وإن شهد بالمال ثلاثة فرجع أحدهم فلا ضمان عليه فإن رجع آخر ضمن الراجعان نصف المال
Two retract: all the money; one of two: half. Of three, one retracting owes nothing (two still stand); if a second retracts, the two owe half.tap to reveal
Man and two women: women retract?
وإن شهد رجل وامرأتان فرجعت امرأة ضمنت ربع الحق وإن رجعنا ضمنتا نصف الحق
One woman retracting: a quarter; both: half. Man and ten women, eight retract: nothing; a ninth: a quarter on the women.tap to reveal
3 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

A court awarded $4,000 to a plaintiff on the testimony of two witnesses. A month later both tell the judge they lied.

The judgment stands, but both owe the $4,000 to the defendant (rj_before, rj_amt). If only one had retracted, he would owe half.discuss first — then tap

Kitab Adab al-Qadi (Judge's Conduct)

This kitāb lays out qualifications and etiquette of the judge: conditions of witness plus ijtihād, not seeking office, taking over records, open sitting, refusing gifts, equal treatment, jailing debtors, women judges (except ḥudūd/qiṣāṣ), judge-to-judge letters, upholding or overturning rulings, and arbitration (taḥkīm) with its limits.
Who may be a judge?
ويكون من أهل الاجتهاد ولا بأس بالدخول في القضاء لمن يثق أنه يؤدي فرضه ويكره الدخول فيه لمن يخاف العجز عنه ولا يأمن على نفسه الحيف فيه ولا ينبغي أن يطلب الولاية ولا يسألها
He must meet the conditions of a witness and be a mujtahid. Entering is permitted for one confident of fulfilling the duty, disliked for one afraid of failing or injustice; he should not seek or ask for office.tap to reveal
What does a new judge do on taking office?
ومن قلد القضاء يسلم إليه ديوان القاضي الذي قبله وينظر في حال المحبوسين فمن اعترف بحق ألزمه إياه
He receives the predecessor's records, reviews prisoners (those who admit a right are bound), and reviews deposits and endowments.tap to reveal
Where does he sit?
ويجلس للحكم جلوسا ظاهرا في المسجد
He sits openly, in the mosque.tap to reveal

Not allowed (aq_hadiya); equal treatment of parties (aq_taswiya).discuss first — then tap

Kitab al-Qisma (Division of Property)

Qisma explains how co-owned property is divided: the divider (qāsim), fee basis, when division can be demanded, what is divisible, how heirs are treated when absent, and the method (sketch, equalize, number, lots). It ends with disputes after division: errors, claims by third parties.
Who divides?
ويجب أن يكون عدلا مأمونا عالما بالقسمة
The imam ideally appoints a divider paid from the treasury so people don't pay; failing that, one paid by those dividing. He must be upright, trustworthy and skilled. The judge does not force people to use one divider nor allow dividers to form a cartel.tap to reveal
Basis of the divider's fee?
وأجر القسمة على عدد الرؤوس عند أبي حنيفة وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد: على قدر الأنصاء
Abū Ḥanīfa: per head. Abū Yūsuf and Muḥammad: per share.tap to reveal
Co-owners claim to be heirs of a deceased and ask for division of real estate?
حتى يقيموا البينة على موته وعدد ورثته
Abū Ḥanīfa: the judge does not divide until they prove death and heirs. Abū Yūsuf and Muḥammad: divides on their admission and records it as by their statement. For non-real-estate goods, all agree he divides; if they claim purchase of real estate, divides; if they claim ownership without saying how, divides.tap to reveal
5 · Case clinic · which bāb, rule, terms?

Three siblings co-own their late father's house; two want to partition, the third refuses.

If each can benefit from his share, any one can demand partition (qs_intifa); shares assigned by lots (qs_quraʿa).discuss first — then tap

Kitab al-Ikrah (Coercion)

Ikrāh explains how duress affects acts. A real threat from one with power makes contracts voidable, allows consuming forbidden food or uttering kufr (with tawriya), allows destroying property (coercer pays), but never killing; divorce and manumission take effect; zinā has differing views; coerced apostasy does not separate spouses.
When does coercion take effect?
الإكراه يثبت حكمه إذا حصل ممن يقدر على إيقاع ما توعد به سلطانا كان أو لصا
When it comes from one able to carry out the threat, whether ruler or robber.tap to reveal
Contracts under threat?
فباع أو اشترى فهو بالخيار: إن شاء أمضى البيع وإن شاء فسخه
Coerced by killing, severe beating or long imprisonment to sell, buy, admit a debt, or lease, he chooses: affirm or rescind.tap to reveal
Receiving the price?
وإن كان قبض الثمن طوعا فقد أجاز البيع وإن كان قبضه مكرها فليس بإجازة وعليه رده إن كان قائما في يده وإن هلك المبيع في يد المشتري وهو غير مكره ضمن قيمته
Receiving the price voluntarily is affirmation; receiving it under coercion is not, and he must return it if still in hand. If the item perishes with the buyer, who is not coerced, he pays its value. The coerced may charge the coercer.tap to reveal

Kitab al-Siyar I: Jihad (conduct)

The first part of Siyar covers the legal basis, who is exempt, defence versus offence, invitation, means and ethics of fighting, truces and treatment of those who convert or come over. It shows Islamic war law as structured by duty levels (collective vs individual) and by protected categories.
What is the ruling of jihād?
الجهاد فرض على الكفاية إذا قام به فريق من الناس سقط عن الباقين وإن لم يقم به أحد أثم جميع الناس بتركه
A collective duty: if a group performs it, the rest are relieved; if none does, all are sinful.tap to reveal
Who is exempt?
وقتال الكفار واجب وإن لم يبدؤونا ولا يجب الجهاد على صبي ولا عبد ولا امرأة ولا أعمى ولا مقعد ولا أقطع
Fighting is obligatory even if they do not start; not obligatory upon a child, slave, woman, blind, lame or one-handed.tap to reveal
When the enemy invades a town?
وإن هجم العدو على بلد وجب على جميع المسلمين الدفع تخرج المرأة بغير إذن زوجها والعبد بغير إذن المولى
Defence is obligatory for all; a woman goes without her husband's permission and a slave without his master's.tap to reveal

A town under attack by an invader; a citizen asks whether his wife needs his permission to join the defence.

Defence is individual duty; she needs no permission (sy_hujum); otherwise she does (sy_idhn).discuss first — then tap

Kitab al-Siyar II: Spoils and Safe-Conduct

This part covers dividing the spoils (when, to whom, the fifth), safe-conduct (who may grant), recovery of seized Muslim property, bonuses (nafl) and spoils of the killed (salab), shares of horsemen/footmen, spending of the fifth, raiding without leave, and the musta'min's stay.
Where is the spoil divided?
ولا يقسم غنيمة في دار الحرب حتى يخرجها إلى دار الإسلام والردء والمقاتل في العسكر سواء
Not divided in enemy land until brought to Dār al-Islām; supporter and fighter are equal.tap to reveal
Reinforcements arriving late?
وإذا لحقهم المدد في دار الحرب قبل أن يخرجوا الغنيمة إلى دار الإسلام شاركوهم فيها ولا حق لأهل سوق العسكر في الغنيمة إلا أن يقاتلوا
They share if they join before taking it out; camp-market people have no right unless they fight.tap to reveal
Who can grant safe-conduct?
وإذا أمن رجل حر أو امرأة كافرا أو جماعة أو أهل حصن أو مدينة صح أمانهم ولم يجز لأحد من المسلمين قتلهم إلا أن يكون في ذلك مفسدة فينبذ إليهم الإمام
A free man or woman may grant it to a person, a group or a town; it is valid unless the imam sees harm and gives notice.tap to reveal

After a year he faces jizya and becomes dhimmī and cannot leave (sb_mustamin).discuss first — then tap

Kitab al-Faraid I: Heirs and Barriers

Farāʾiḍ is the Qurʾānic science of dividing an estate. Qudūrī starts with who is an heir: ten males and seven females agreed upon, and four barriers (slavery, killing, apostasy, difference of religion). This is the gateway: first decide who is eligible, then who gets a fixed share, who takes the remainder (ʿaṣaba), who blocks whom (ḥajb), and how to compute it.
Which ten males are agreed upon as heirs?
المجمع على توريثهم من الذكور عشرة: الابن وابن الابن وإن سفل والأب والجد أب الأب وإن علا والأخ وابن الأخ والعم وابن العم والزوج ومولى النعمة
Son; son's son however low; father; grandfather (father's father) however high; brother; brother's son; paternal uncle; uncle's son; husband; the freeing master.tap to reveal
Which seven females are agreed heirs?
ومن الإناث سبع: البنت وبنت الابن والأم والجدة والأخت والزوجة ومولاة النعمة
Daughter; son's daughter; mother; grandmother; sister; wife; the freeing mistress.tap to reveal
Who never inherits?
ولا يرث أربعة: المملوك والقاتل من المقتول والمرتد وأهل الملتين
Four: the slave; the killer from his victim; the apostate; and people of two religions (one from the other).tap to reveal

Kitab al-Faraid II: The Six Fixed Shares

This section is the engine of farāʾiḍ: the six Qurʾānic fractions and who takes each. Learn it as holder tables: half (5 holders), quarter (husband with child; wives without), eighth (wives with child), two-thirds (two or more of the half-category, not husband), third (mother, uterine siblings, ʿUmariyyatān), sixth (seven holders). Then the rules of exclusion for grandmothers, uterine siblings, granddaughters and paternal sisters.
What are the fixed shares?
والفروض المحدودة في كتاب الله تعالى ستة: النصف والربع والثمن والثلثان والثلث والسدس
Six: 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, 1/6.tap to reveal
Who takes one-half?
والزوج إذا لم يكن للميت ولد ولا ولد ابن
Half belongs to: the husband (if no child or son's child); a single daughter; a single son's daughter (if no daughter); a full sister alone; a paternal sister alone (if no full sister). [The printed line is partly corrupt; the list is completed from the standard ruling.]tap to reveal
Who takes one-quarter?
والربع: فرض الزوج مع الولد أو ولد الابن والزوجات إذا لم يكن للميت ولد ولا ولد ابن
The husband takes a quarter when the wife left a child or son's child; wives take a quarter (shared among them, not each) when the husband left none.tap to reveal

Kitab al-Faraid III: Asaba and Hajb

After the fixed shares, the remainder goes to the ʿaṣaba in order of nearness: descendants, father, grandfather, brothers, uncles, then the freeing master. Males and females of the same degree share 2:1. Exclusion (ḥajb) removes or reduces: a child or two siblings reduce the mother; grandchildren take what daughters leave. Special cases: ibnā ʿamm, the Mushtaraka, and the grandfather-siblings dispute.
What is the order of ʿaṣabāt?
وأقرب العصبات البنون ثم بنوهم ثم الأب ثم الجد ثم بنو الأب وهم الإخوة ثم بنو الجد وهم الأعمام ثم بنوا أب الجد
Sons, then their sons, then father, then grandfather, then sons of the father (brothers), then sons of the grandfather (uncles), then sons of the great-grandfather.tap to reveal
When same degree?
وإذا استوى بنو أب في الدرجة فأولاهم من كان لأب وأم
Among equal degree, the full (both parents) is first.tap to reveal
Who shares as male = two females?
والابن وابن الابن والإخوة يقاسمون أخواتهم للذكر مثل حظ الأنثيين ومن عداهم من العصبات ينفرد بالميراث ذكورهم دون إناثهم
Son, son's son, brothers share with their sisters: male double. Other ʿaṣabāt (uncles etc.): males alone.tap to reveal

Bab Hisab al-Faraid II: Tashih and Munasakha

When a group's shares do not divide evenly, the root is corrected. Break on one group: multiply by the head count (coprime) or wafq (agreeing). Several groups: tamāthul, tadākhul, tawāfuq, tabāyun. Then distribute the estate and handle munāsakha (an heir dies first).
What if shares do not divide evenly?
فإذا انقسمت المسألة على الورثة فقد صحت وإن لم تنقسم سهام فريق عليهم فاضرب عددهم في أصل المسألة وعولها إن كانت عائلة فما خرج فمنه تصح المسألة كامرأة وأخوين: للمرأة الربع سهم وللأخوين ما بقي وهي ثلاثة أسهم لا تنقسم عليهما فاضرب اثنين في أصل المسألة فتكون ثمانية ومنها تصح
If divides, correct. If a group's shares do not divide on them, multiply their head count by the root (or awl total); result is the corrected root. Example: wife + 2 brothers: root 4: wife 1, brothers 3 (cannot split by 2): 2 × 4 = 8.tap to reveal
If shares and heads share a factor?
وإن وافق سهامهم عددهم فاضرب وفق عددهم في أصل المسألة كامرأة وستة إخوة للمرأة الربع سهم وللإخوة ثلاثة فاضرب وفق عددهم في أصل المسألة يكون ثمانية ومنه تصح
Multiply the wafq of heads by the root: wife + 6 brothers: shares 3 vs heads 6 agree by a third; wafq 2; 2 × 4 = 8.tap to reveal
Several groups break?

1Promising bonus?
Allowed during fighting; he may say 'who kills has his spoils' or 'a quarter after the fifth'. After securing spoils, nafl only from the fifth.
2Where does he sit?
He sits openly, in the mosque.
3Two people dispute an animal; one rides it, the other holds the bridle?
The rider is preferred; likewise the one with the load on a camel, and the one wearing a shirt over the one holding its sleeve.
4Root 12?
A quarter with a third or sixth: root 12; rises to 13, 15, 17 (only odd).
5Mixed weave?
Permitted when the warp is silk and weft cotton or khazz.
6Mass deaths?
Each one's estate goes to his living heirs; they do not inherit from each other.
7Forced to utter kufr?
With a threat to life or limb, he may utter it with tawriya, heart at ease with faith, without sin; if he endures to death without uttering, he is rewarded (p. 220).
8Withdrawal?
From his slave-girl without permission; from wife only with her permission.
9Ruling and limits?
Not obligatory but recommended; not to an heir unless heirs approve; not beyond a third, not to the killer.
10Bequeathing a son's share?
'His son's share' is invalid (it is the son's property); 'like his son's share' valid: with two sons, the legatee gets a third.
